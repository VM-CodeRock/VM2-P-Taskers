Industry News and Developments

CISA CIRCIA Rulemaking Town Halls Scheduled for June 15-18, 2026

DHS's Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency has announced a consolidated schedule of four virtual town halls in June to gather stakeholder input on the final CIRCIA rule, which will mandate cyber incident and ransom payment reporting for critical infrastructure owners and operators. Town halls run June 15-18, 11:30 AM to 3:30 PM ET. Registration closes two business days before each session. This accelerates the compliance services market for federal contractors supporting covered entities.

Source: PilieroMazza Weekly Update, May 28, 2026 · InsideCybersecurity, May 22, 2026

SANS 2026 Survey: Only 1 in 3 Government Cybersecurity Programs Are Fully Funded

A survey of 417 federal, state, and local cybersecurity practitioners released May 28 found that 63% cite budget limitations as their primary obstacle. Only 22% rate themselves capable of executing their security strategy at scale, despite 55% having a fully implemented strategy. The gap is in execution capacity — workforce development, automation, and technology integration. Threat detection and response (40%) and staff training (41%) are the most resource-constrained functions. This creates sustained demand for cybersecurity managed services, training, and tool integration support from contractors.

Source: SANS Institute, May 28, 2026

OPM Proposes Governmentwide NDA Requirement for Federal Employees

The Office of Personnel Management published a draft rule in the Federal Register proposing a governmentwide nondisclosure agreement for all federal civil servants. Under the proposal, failure to sign could result in removal from federal service. The measure adds another compliance layer to federal employee relations and workforce management — relevant to Changeis HR consulting (541612) and organizational support capabilities. Public comment period closes June 26, 2026.

Source: E&E News / POLITICO, May 27, 2026

FAA Surges Medical Staff 40% Following Whistleblower Allegations

The Federal Aviation Administration has added 40% more staff to review health certifications for pilots and air traffic controllers after a whistleblower alleged that one-in-three medical officer roles were vacant and workloads had risen 250% over seven years. FAA also contracted out some administrative work for ATC medical approvals. The staffing surge and administrative contracting-out creates potential opportunities for workforce management, program support, and IT-enabled workflow modernization services at FAA — consistent with EY's recent FAA AHR Post Organization Support award.

Source: Government Executive, May 26, 2026

House Small Business Committee Advances "Rule of Two" Codification Bill

H.R. 2804, the Protecting Small Business Competitions Act of 2025, was unanimously advanced by the House Small Business Committee. The bill would codify the longstanding Rule of Two into statute, requiring agencies to set contracts aside for small businesses whenever two or more qualified small businesses can perform the work at a fair price. If enacted, this strengthens Changeis's competitive position on upcoming solicitations in CISA, USCIS, and USCG markets where it qualifies as a small business.

Source: PilieroMazza Weekly Update, May 28, 2026
