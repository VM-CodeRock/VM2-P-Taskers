Executive Summary — Top 3 Takeaways
Takeaway 1

OASIS+ Phase II Awards Live — Ordering Window Open

GSA issued the first OASIS+ Phase II apparent awardee notifications on May 12 and formal awards/NTPs on ~May 22. The vehicle is now available for ordering via eBuy. Rolling awards continue. Changeis must evaluate which pools align with its socioeconomic status and act on domains (Management & Advisory, Enterprise Solutions, Technical & Engineering) within reach.

Takeaway 2

MAS Refresh 32 Imminent — AI Clause & Pricing 2.0 Coming

MAS Refresh 32 is expected in June 2026 and will include the postponed "American AI" GSAR clause (552.239-7001), DEI EO language, drone/UAS terms, and new SIN 518210GM for Grants QSMO. Pricing 2.0 launched June 5 — tightening MAS market thresholds for products. Services Plus File discount field went optional May 19.

Takeaway 3

OneGov Hits $1.1B Savings; Snowflake Deal Signals AI Data Push

OneGov marked its one-year anniversary with $1.1B in taxpayer savings (April 29) and added Snowflake as its 21st agreement on May 21 — up to 50% compute discounts for federal agencies. With AI tools from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Perplexity already on OneGov, this is GSA's clearest signal yet that AI data infrastructure is a procurement priority for FY27.

Policy / FAR Watch
GSAR 552.239-7001 "American AI Clause" — Expected in MAS Refresh 32 (June 2026)
Status as of June 8, 2026 | Source: Winvale, Wiley Rein

The proposed GSAR clause governing AI systems in GSA Schedule contracts was delayed from Refresh 31 (comment deadline extended to April 3, 2026) and is now expected in Refresh 32. Key requirements: (1) use only "American AI Systems" — defined per Advancing American AI Act; (2) disclose all AI systems within 30 days of award; (3) government owns all Custom Developments and data outputs; (4) contractors cannot use government data to train/improve AI models; (5) contractors responsible for AI Service Provider compliance even if not a subcontractor; (6) 72-hour incident reporting to CISA; (7) 30-day notice before changing AI Service Provider; (8) systems must follow "Unbiased AI Principles" — no DEI-aligned bias.

Changeis Impact: Any AI capability offered under MAS must comply. Assess current AI tools (vendor nationality, data handling, model change procedures). Negotiate commercial agreements with AI providers to enable government data segregation. This will likely be a 60-day implementation window once Refresh 32 issues.

Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO) — Phase 2 Deviations Rolling; Major Thresholds Change June 30
Ongoing through June 30, 2026 | Source: US Federal Contractor Registration, Open FAR Cases (June 5, 2026)

Major thresholds take effect for contracts awarded after June 30, 2026: (1) TINA threshold raises from $2M to $10M — eliminates certified cost/pricing data on most professional services orders; (2) CAS full coverage threshold raises from $50M to $100M collective annual awards; (3) CAS per-contract trigger raises from $2.5M to $35M — significant relief for mid-size contractors. Protest payment withholding (5%) still expected via DFARS by June 2026.

Changeis Impact: Review any contracts awarded after June 30 to determine TINA/CAS applicability — substantial accounting relief likely. Update compliance posture and internal thresholds tracking. Contracting officers may exercise more discretion under the new plain-language FAR framework.

MAS Refresh 31 — TDR Now Mandatory for ALL SINs (Effective April 2, 2026)
Effective April 2, 2026 | Source: FEDSched

TDR is now mandatory across all GSA MAS SINs. Key impacts: Price Reductions Clause eliminated; Basis of Award and customer sales tracking eliminated; all non-TDR proposals rejected; existing non-TDR contractors must accept "Participate in TDR" mass modification. Startup Springboard reverted to IT/FASt Lane only (2022 expansion reversed). Additional TDR reporting elements pending Sales Reporting Portal update. OLM SIN now open to all MAS contractors.

Changeis Impact: Confirm TDR mass mod has been accepted. Begin capturing transactional-level sales data per the new reporting requirements. If pursuing new MAS contract, proposals must be TDR-compliant.

EO 14240 — GSA as Executive Agent for GWACs; Agencies Must Use Existing Vehicles First
Ongoing — Updated guidance as of May 2026 | Source: Mayer Brown

FAR has been updated per EO 14240 to require agencies to use existing government-wide contracts before creating new vehicles. GSA designated as executive agent for all IT GWACs. Best-in-Class contracts (Alliant 3, OASIS+, Polaris) at top of priority stack. Agencies must seek head-of-agency exception to bypass. OneGov OEM agreements further pressure resellers and traditional SI primes.

Changeis Impact: All BD strategy must route through Alliant 3, OASIS+, MAS, Polaris, or STARS III. Standalone IDIQs and BPA-first strategies are no longer viable for most requirements. Teaming strategy with GWAC primes is essential where Changeis lacks direct access.
