Executive Summary

The week of June 10, 2026 delivered a dense burst of FedCiv contract activity driven primarily by three macro forces: the June 2 Executive Order on AI Innovation and Security (EO 14409) forcing rapid CISA/DHS/OPM action within 30–60 days; the Secure America Act passing Congress and locking in multi-year ICE/CBP/DHS funding through FY2029; and OPM's landmark Oracle Federal HR 2.0 award ($396M, 10-year) consolidating 100+ federal HR systems.

For Changeis, the most actionable signals are in: (1) DHS/ICE cyber-analytics procurement (ICE–TRM Labs HSTF award, $50–100M, sub-contracting possible); (2) VA cloud brokerage RFI (RFP expected ~Q3 FY26, multi-cloud FinOps + AI-enabled governance); (3) GSA EIS network modernization follow-on work; and (4) the CISA AI-enabled cyber tools mandate creating near-term consulting/implementation demand across all civilian FCEB agencies within 30 days of EO.

The FAA ATC modernization program ($12.5B baseline, additional software funding pending Congress) and the DOT Penn Station/NEC redevelopment (~$5B NEC rail investment) are medium-term BD targets worth monitoring for IT/PMO subcontract opportunities.

FedCiv News Highlights — Last 7 Days
  • EO: Promoting Advanced AI Innovation and Security (June 2, 2026) — President Trump signed EO 14409, directing CISA, DHS, and OPM to deploy AI-enabled cyber tools across all federal civilian executive branch (FCEB) networks within 30 days, and establish an AI Cybersecurity Clearinghouse. Companion NSPM issued June 5 on AI in the National Security Enterprise. High BD signal for Changeis: cybersecurity + AI implementation demand across every FedCiv agency.
  • OPM Awards Oracle Federal HR 2.0 — $396M, 10-Year (June 10, 2026) — Oracle wins the first-ever governmentwide Core Human Capital Management contract, consolidating 100+ HR systems for 2M federal employees. Structured as firm-fixed-price; core implementation target: fall 2026. Requirements include FISMA/FedRAMP compliance, workforce analytics, and self-service capabilities. Sub-contracting, change management, and data migration opportunities.
  • ICE–TRM Labs Sole-Source HSTF Analytical Support ($50–100M, June 10) — ICE/HSI announced intent to award TRM Labs a sole-source firm-fixed-price contract for forensic software and analytical services supporting the Homeland Security Task Force's Cyber Disruption Center (Fairfax, VA). Performance through July 2027. Capability statements due June 11.
  • Secure America Act Passes — DHS/ICE/CBP Funded Through FY2029 (June 10) — Reconciliation legislation guarantees multiyear funding for CBP personnel, border screening/security technology, ICE IT modernization, facility sustainment, and 287(g) agreements. Creates a predictable procurement pipeline for DHS IT modernization through FY2029.
  • Bipartisan "Great American AI Act" Discussion Draft (June 4–5) — Draft legislation would codify NIST's Center for AI Standards and Innovation ($100M/yr, FY27–29), mandate AI-enabled open-source security grants via CISA, and authorize a national AI testbed (DOE/NIST/NSF). Preempts state AI laws. Reinforces federal AI standards demand.
  • GSA Acknowledges AI Catch-Up Gap (June 10) — GSA Administrator Forst at IBM Think Gov 2026 acknowledged GSA is "at the doorstep" of AI deployment and called for industry engagement to close the modernization gap. VA is simultaneously pursuing a cloud brokerage RFI (responses due June 22) for AI-enabled multi-cloud FinOps.
  • ICE–Whooster Data Analysis Follow-On Forecast (June 9) — FedScoop reports ICE/HSI forecasts a $1M, 3-year follow-on with Whooster for mobile device investigative data analysis (Q4 FY26 award expected). Signals continuing ICE investment in data/analytics tools.
  • FAA ATC Modernization Seeks Additional Congressional Funding (June 10) — With $12.5B committed, FAA requests more from Congress for software development to transform airspace management. Next-phase software contracts will follow if funding secured.
  • MetTel Completes GSA EIS Network Upgrades (June 10) — MetTel completed SD-WAN and VoIP upgrades across 11 GSA offices under the $50B EIS contract. Return-to-work mandate driving incremental network modernization task orders governmentwide.
  • HHS Posts AI Pilot RFI (June 8) — HHS issued an RFI for an advanced AI pilot focused on frontier models, agentic capabilities, and enterprise scaling. Signals future HHS solicitation for large-scale AI deployment support.
