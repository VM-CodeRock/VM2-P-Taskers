1. Federal AI Policy & Acquisition
GSA · Anthropic · August 27 Deadline Deadline
GSA Artificial Intelligence Page Confirms: All Anthropic System Integrations Removed by August 27, 2026

GSA's official artificial-intelligence procurement page carries a standing alert: "GSA will remove any system integrations with Anthropic by Aug. 27, 2026." This follows the February 27, 2026 presidential directive to immediately cease Anthropic use, GSA's initial removal of Anthropic from USAi.gov and the Multiple Award Schedule, a March 26 federal court preliminary injunction (N.D. Cal., 26-cv-01996-RFL) that temporarily restored access and forced GSA to reinstate Anthropic technology, and continuing litigation over the underlying supply-chain-risk designation. (GSA.gov; Congressional Research Service, May 5)

What This Means For Changeis

(1) The August 27 date is now the operative planning horizon for any Changeis federal proposal that assumes Anthropic system integration continuity beyond that date — flag it in every active bid narrative referencing Claude within federal delivery architecture. (2) The injunction/removal cycle (removed Feb 27, restored Apr, removal reconfirmed for Aug 27) signals continued legal instability; build contingency language into proposals identifying an alternate model provider (OpenAI, Google, or Perplexity) as a fallback. (3) Monitor for any further court action between now and August 27 that could again pause the removal.

CISA · NSA · Mythos Federal Security
CISA's Attack Surface Evaluation Team Uses Anthropic's Restricted Mythos Model to Scan Federal Code; NSA Also Reported Using It Despite Blacklist

Reuters reported that CISA is using Anthropic's AI model Mythos to audit government software, with the scanning performed by CISA's Attack Surface Evaluation team, the unit that runs digital security assessments and simulated hacking exercises across federal systems. Two sources said the audits have already uncovered a large number of vulnerabilities, though Reuters could not confirm the volume of code reviewed or bug severity. The NSA has separately been reported using Mythos despite the model's broader government blacklist status. (Reuters, Jul 6; SecurityBrief US, Jul 10; Mallory, Jul 7)

What This Means For Changeis

(1) This is a live contradiction Changeis can cite in federal AI advisory work: the same agencies enforcing Anthropic removal from procurement vehicles are operationally dependent on Anthropic's most capable model for cybersecurity mission work. (2) CISA's Mythos use is a signal that cyber-focused federal clients will continue seeking Annex A/Glasswing-style trusted-partner access regardless of procurement-vehicle status — position Changeis cybersecurity practice teams to advise on this access-versus-procurement gap. (3) Track whether GSA's August 27 system-integration removal carves out an exception for CISA/NSA mission use, similar to prior white-house guidance drafts exempting Mythos from the supply-chain-risk designation.

Perplexity · GSA OneGov · MAS
Perplexity Enterprise Pro for Government Remains at $0.25/Agency for 15 Months; One of Two GSA AI-Prioritization Designees

Perplexity's government hub confirms it is "in active discussions with federal officials to offer Perplexity Enterprise Pro for Government through government-wide vehicles such as the Multiple Award Schedule, at $0.25 per agency for the first 15 months," pursuant to GSA's OneGov strategy. Perplexity's Enterprise Pro platform holds GSA's AI Prioritization designation, one of only two AI services with that status, which is intended to expedite Perplexity's FedRAMP authorization through the cloud-native 20x pathway. Since November 2025, all traffic identified as originating from a U.S. government agency automatically receives zero-data-retention protections at no cost, without requiring a contract. (Perplexity, Jul 8; GSA Buy AI; MeriTalk)

What This Means For Changeis

(1) Perplexity's federal pricing and zero-data-retention default make it a low-friction alternative for any Changeis proposal seeking to avoid the Anthropic procurement uncertainty described above. (3) Track GSA's Buy AI page (currently listing Anthropic at $1 through Aug 2026 and Google Gemini for Government at $0.47 through Sep 2026) for the date Perplexity formally appears on that pricing table.

2. Apple v. OpenAI Trade Secrets Litigation
Apple · OpenAI · Trade Secrets Breaking
Apple Sues OpenAI, io Products, and Two Former Apple Employees for Systematic Trade Secret Theft

Apple filed a 41-page complaint in federal court in the Northern District of California on July 10, accusing OpenAI, its hardware-design unit io Products (co-founded by former Apple design chief Jony Ive), OpenAI Chief Hardware Officer Tang Tan (a former Apple product design VP who led iPhone, Apple Watch, and AirPods development), and former Apple hardware engineer Chang Liu of a coordinated effort to steal confidential Apple data. The complaint alleges Chang Liu retained access to Apple's cloud file storage after leaving the company due to a security gap tied to a retained company laptop, and downloaded dozens of confidential hardware files after joining OpenAI in January, including unreleased product information, engineering demo decks, technical specifications, and supply-chain vendor details. Apple's filing states OpenAI's "nascent hardware business now rests on the shakiest of foundations, rotten to its core by its illegal reliance on misappropriated trade secrets," and discloses that more than 400 former Apple employees currently work at OpenAI. OpenAI responded: "We have no interest in other companies' trade secrets. We remain focused on building innovative technology that empowers people everywhere." (Fortune, Jul 13; TechCrunch, Jul 13; Reuters, Jul 13)

Apple seeks monetary damages, an injunction ordering OpenAI to stop the alleged conduct, destruction of all proprietary materials, and a redesign of any upcoming OpenAI hardware product to remove Apple-derived technology. Analysts note the suit's practical impact will land well before any final ruling: it threatens to slow OpenAI's hiring pipeline and hardware product plans, and introduces legal uncertainty as OpenAI has confidentially filed IPO paperwork targeting a Nasdaq listing. Bloomberg Intelligence expects Apple could win early, targeted relief isolating disputed material and forcing evidence preservation well before trial. (The Next Web, Jul 13)

What This Means For Changeis

(1) This litigation is a live case study for any Changeis IP-protection or insider-threat advisory engagement — the alleged laptop-retention access gap is a concrete control failure worth referencing in cybersecurity hygiene materials. (2) OpenAI hardware timeline risk (previously targeting a 2026 announcement, 2027 launch) should be flagged as uncertain in any Changeis analysis referencing OpenAI's consumer device roadmap. (3) The IPO-timing overhang is relevant context for any Changeis client evaluating OpenAI as a long-term federal or enterprise AI vendor given near-term corporate uncertainty.

3. Anthropic & Claude
Fable 5 · Pricing · Extension
Anthropic Extends Free Fable 5 Access a Second Time to July 19 After Billing Backlash; Claude Code 50% Limit Boost Extended in Parallel

Anthropic pushed the included (no-extra-cost) Fable 5 access deadline for paid subscribers from July 12 to July 19, 2026 at 11:59:59 PM PT — the second extension after the original July 7 cutoff. Pro, Max, Team, and select legacy-seat Enterprise users continue to receive Fable 5 access within 50% of their weekly usage limits at no additional cost through the new date; standard Enterprise and API users, who never had included access, see no change. The Claude Code 50% higher weekly-limit promotion was extended on the identical schedule, automatically applied with no user action required. The extension followed heavy user backlash after subscribers shared screenshots of unexpectedly large credit bills once the original window lapsed, with some warning they would switch to GPT-5.6 Sol or Grok 4.5. (Digital Applied, Jul 13; Help Net Security, Jul 13)

Anthropic spokesperson Reem Ateyeh said on July 9 that Fable will return to standard subscription plans "as quickly as we can" once capacity allows, without giving a firm date or capacity threshold. After July 19, continued Fable 5 access on subscription plans reverts to usage-credit billing at $10/$50 per million input/output tokens, unchanged from July 1 terms, with a $2,000 daily redemption cap and available auto-reload. (36Kr, Jul 14)

What This Means For Changeis

Budget Fable 5 as a usage-credit model beyond July 19 for any Changeis engagement, not a subscription-included model; monitor Anthropic's support articles for a third extension, which the repeated pattern (July 7 to 12 to 19) suggests remains plausible given ongoing capacity constraints.

Anthropic · India · Pricing
Anthropic Begins Localizing Claude Pricing for India, Its Second-Largest Market

Anthropic has started rolling out India-localized pricing on Claude's website and mobile apps: Claude Pro at approximately ₹2,000 (about $21) per month billed annually versus $17 in the U.S.; Claude Max starting at ₹11,999 (about $125) versus $100 in the U.S.; and Team plans starting at ₹2,399 (about $25) per seat versus $20 in the U.S., with India prices including local taxes. Anthropic has not yet enabled Unified Payments Interface (UPI) support, unlike OpenAI, which added rupee pricing with UPI support in 2025. India accounts for 5.8% of global Claude usage, Anthropic's second-largest market after the U.S. Anthropic opened a Bengaluru office in February and appointed former Microsoft India managing director Irina Ghose to lead its India business in January. (TechCrunch, Jul 13)

What This Means For Changeis

India localization signals Anthropic's continued global commercial expansion despite ongoing U.S. federal procurement uncertainty — useful context when advising clients on Anthropic's overall business durability independent of the federal relationship.

Mythos 5.1 · Rumor · Unconfirmed
Unverified Leak Claims Anthropic Has Trained an Unreleased Mythos 5.1/6 Successor; Anthropic Has Not Confirmed

A report from TheWinCentral claims Anthropic may have already trained an unreleased model more capable than Claude Mythos 5, possibly designated Mythos 5.1 or Mythos 6, based on an unspecified leak with no benchmark data, product identifier, or independent corroboration. Anthropic has not confirmed the existence, training completion, or intentional withholding of any such model. Mythos 5 was announced June 9, 2026 as a restricted model for approved cybersecurity and life-sciences partners under Anthropic's Project Glasswing trusted-access program; Fable 5 is the broadly available configuration with additional safety classifiers. (WindowsForum, Jul 13)

4. OpenAI
GPT-5.6 · ChatGPT Work · GPT-Live Model Launch
GPT-5.6 Reaches General Availability After Government Cybersecurity Review; ChatGPT Work Agent and GPT-Live Voice Model Launch Alongside

OpenAI's GPT-5.6 family (Sol, Terra, Luna) is now generally available following a limited preview period and after the Trump administration's cybersecurity review greenlit public rollout of the model suite announced June 26. OpenAI says GPT-5.6 Sol "sets a new standard for both intelligence and efficiency, achieving state-of-the-art results across coding, knowledge work, cybersecurity, and science while outperforming previous and competing frontier models with fewer tokens and at lower estimated cost." Sol is priced at $5 per million input tokens and $30 per million output — roughly half Anthropic Fable 5's $10/$50 rate. (The Verge, Jul 13; Marketing AI Institute, Jul 13; Slow AI, Jul 13)

OpenAI also introduced ChatGPT Work, an agent that gathers information across a user's apps and workflows to produce finished sheets, slides, docs, and web apps, and can stay on complex multi-step projects for hours by breaking them into independent sub-tasks; it is powered by GPT-5.6 with Codex capabilities built in. GPT-Live, a new full-duplex voice model family, can listen and speak simultaneously, using conversational cues like "mhmm" to signal active listening; it now powers ChatGPT Voice. OpenAI reportedly offered the U.S. government a 5% equity stake as part of the arrangement enabling GPT-5.6's launch, with the White House confirming the company did not legally require formal approval to launch. (AP News, Jul 13)

What This Means For Changeis

ChatGPT Work is a direct competitor to Claude Cowork and Perplexity's agentic offerings — update any Changeis agent-tooling comparison matrix to include it; the GPT-5.6/Fable 5 price gap (roughly half) is a relevant data point for cost-sensitive federal proposal budgeting.

5. Google DeepMind
Gemini 3.5 Pro · DiffusionGemma
Gemini 3.5 Pro GA Remains Set for July 17; Google Ships Open-Weight DiffusionGemma 26B Text-Diffusion Model

Gemini 3.5 Pro's general-availability launch remains slated for July 17, 2026 after slipping from an original June target for additional quality refinements following enterprise preview testing; Google has not confirmed final pricing, with estimates ranging from roughly $2/$12 to $15/$60 per million input/output tokens. Gemini 3.5 Flash is already generally available as the default model for the Gemini app and AI Mode in Search globally. (Enterprise DNA, Jul 8; Tech Insider)

Separately, Google released DiffusionGemma, an experimental open-weight (Apache 2.0) 26B-parameter mixture-of-experts model that generates entire blocks of text simultaneously rather than token-by-token, built on the Gemma 4 family and incorporating research from Gemini Diffusion. Google says the model can reach speeds of 1,000+ tokens per second on an Nvidia H100 and 700+ tokens per second on a GeForce RTX 5090 — up to 4x faster than standard autoregressive decoding on GPU hardware, though with lower output quality than the standard Gemma 4 model. Google CEO Sundar Pichai said the model targets latency-sensitive workflows such as inline editing and rapid iteration rather than production-quality output. (36Kr, Jul 14)

What This Means For Changeis

Track the July 17 Gemini 3.5 Pro GA date for any Changeis proposal referencing Google Cloud AI capabilities; DiffusionGemma's open-weight Apache 2.0 license makes it relevant for any Changeis technical evaluation of low-latency, self-hosted inference options.

6. xAI, Meta & Agent Tooling
Grok 4.5 · SpaceXAI
xAI Rebrands to SpaceXAI After SpaceX Merger, Ships Grok 4.5 Undercutting GPT-5.6 and Fable 5 on Price; Security Analysis Flags Grok Build CLI Data Exposure

Following its formal merger with SpaceX and rebrand to SpaceXAI, the company released Grok 4.5 on July 8, built in collaboration with Cursor (acquired by SpaceX earlier in 2026). Elon Musk described it as "an Opus-class model, but faster" — roughly at Claude Opus 4.8 capability with faster inference. Grok 4.5 is priced at $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output, about one-fifth of GPT-5.6 Sol's cost and roughly one-eighth of Fable 5's. (Zemith, Jul 13; Slow AI, Jul 13)

A security researcher's wire-level analysis of Grok Build CLI version 0.2.93, published as a GitHub Gist and widely discussed on Hacker News, found the coding agent uploads file contents — including unredacted .env secrets files with API keys and database passwords — to xAI servers via both a live model-turn channel and a separate persisted-storage channel, and independently uploads entire repository snapshots regardless of what code the agent actually processes. A 12GB test repository generated 5.1GiB of uploads to a Google Cloud Storage bucket called grok-code-session-traces. (Developers Digest, Jul 13)

What This Means For Changeis

(1) Grok 4.5's price point makes it worth including in any Changeis cost-comparison matrix for federal or commercial coding-agent proposals. (2) The Grok Build CLI secrets-exposure finding is a concrete example for any Changeis cybersecurity advisory on coding-agent data-handling risk — recommend clients audit .env and credential handling before adopting any third-party coding agent, including Grok Build.

Meta · Muse Image · Muse Spark 1.1
Meta Pulls Muse Image Instagram Remix Feature After Consent Backlash; Muse Spark 1.1 Tops MCP Atlas Tool-Use Benchmark and Launches Meta's First Paid Model API

Meta disabled the public-account remix feature of its Muse Image model on Instagram just three days after launch, following backlash from talent groups CAA and SAG-AFTRA over consent and privacy concerns; the feature had let users generate AI images and video using other public Instagram accounts, with users opted in by default unless their account was private. Meta said the feature is "no longer available" on Instagram while the underlying Muse Image model remains in place. A separate Reuters analysis found Meta's own AI-detection tool failed to identify some of its own AI-generated images once cropped. (International Business Times Australia, Jul 14; Reuters, Jul 13)

Meta Superintelligence Labs, under Alexandr Wang, released Muse Spark 1.1 on July 9 — a multimodal agentic model with a 1,048,576-token context window that scored 88.1 on the MCP Atlas tool-use benchmark, ranking ahead of rival models, and topped JobBench professional tool-use and Humanity's Last Exam leaderboards per third-party trackers. The release marks Meta's first paid model API, charging $1.25 per million input tokens and $4.25 per million output tokens — roughly 25% of rival pricing — a first real push to monetize its AI models externally. Meta shares rose sharply following the release, with JPMorgan analysis noting "signs of an AI product cycle" while remaining Neutral on the stock overall. (Labellerr, Jul 13; MarketBeat, Jul 13)

What This Means For Changeis

The Muse Image consent failure is a useful case study for any Changeis client-facing AI-governance materials on default-opt-in risk; Muse Spark 1.1's low-cost paid API and strong MCP/tool-use benchmarks make it worth evaluating for any Changeis agent-tooling cost comparison.

MCP · Agent Governance
MCP Governance Emerges as Distinct Enterprise Security Category; JetStream Launches Verified MCP Catalog

JetStream Security announced new capabilities combining its JetStream Verified MCP catalog with the JetStream AI Hub, giving enterprise security, risk, and assurance teams a single platform to verify Model Context Protocol server images, control agent permissions, and govern runtime activity. The catalog includes more than 100 JetStream Verified MCP images that are analyzed, hardened, and cryptographically attested before an agent can call them, alongside discovery of shadow MCP servers running outside central oversight. Separately, IANS Research is hosting a July 15 virtual symposium on MCP risks and opportunities, reflecting growing enterprise security-team focus on the protocol as it becomes standard infrastructure for AI-to-system connectivity. (Newswire, Jul 13; IANS Research)

What This Means For Changeis

MCP governance is a nascent but fast-growing advisory category; the combination of the JetStream launch, the Grok Build CLI secrets-exposure finding above, and rising shadow-MCP-server risk supports building a Changeis MCP security advisory offering targeting federal and enterprise clients adopting agentic AI.
