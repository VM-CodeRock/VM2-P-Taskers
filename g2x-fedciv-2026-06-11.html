Executive Summary

June 11, 2026 brought a targeted burst of new FedCiv procurement and policy signals that build directly on yesterday's intelligence. The most consequential new development is CISA's announcement of a $100M+ competitive cyber technology services contract for threat hunting and incident response — the first tangible procurement action arising from EO 14409's 30-day mandate. Simultaneously, CISA confirmed it is imminent on issuing a new Cyber AI Directive for all FCEB agencies, with a new government-wide AI platform to be stood up. Together, these represent the first dollar-value contracting materializations of last week's EO.

Two additional structural shifts emerged: (1) NIH NITAAC announced the sunset of all GWACs by Oct. 29, 2026 (CIO-SP3, CIO-SP3 SB, CIO-CS), with program functions transferring to GSA — this reshapes the IT contracting vehicle landscape and forces immediate re-evaluation of teaming and task order strategies; and (2) SBA issued a sweeping proposed rule to eliminate the 8(a) racial/ethnic presumption of social disadvantage for individually owned firms, opening the program to essentially any US citizen who can document material harm from DEI-era policies.

On the DHS front, Secretary Mullin testified on active contract reviews and CISA staffing, creating uncertainty for DHS incumbent contractors; the House Appropriations DHS funding bill advanced along party lines, cutting CISA funding and TSA appropriations. The carry-forward items — VA Cloud Brokerage RFI (due June 22), CISA BODs (due July 2), OPM/Oracle sub-contracting window, and ICE/TRM Labs award — remain fully actionable.
