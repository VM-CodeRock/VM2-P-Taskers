This is a directly relevant, high-fit opportunity for Changeis. Changeis has directly analogous past performance across DOT/FMCSA, DOT/FHWA, FAA, and Volpe Center task orders covering program management, PRISM/financial management, Agile/Scrum delivery, and portfolio oversight — all of which map precisely to the six labor categories required. The RFTO covers an ITS JPO EPMO task order supporting DOT's Intelligent Transportation Systems Joint Program Office. The contractor will run portfolio scheduling, financial management (PRISM/G-Invoicing), Agile-based program oversight, dashboard and IMS reporting, and procurement closeout for the ITS JPO's ~$100M research portfolio. Six labor categories are required, four of which are Key Personnel. Changeis holds prior work at both FHWA and FMCSA (via Volpe) and multiple FAA EPMO-type task orders that directly address every subtask in the PWS.
