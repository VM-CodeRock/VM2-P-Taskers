1. Executive Summary

The Transportation Security Administration (TSA) is forecasting a full recompete of its Developmental Testing Support Services (DTSS) contract, anticipated for award in Q4 FY2026 with a base + options performance period running through August 2031. The incumbent, Battelle Memorial Institute, has held this work since at least 2021 under BPA 70T04021A7672N002 (task order 70T04021F7672N052, $34.7M). The new IDC is sized at $50–100M over five years, representing a significant expansion from the current obligation. The work supports TSA's Systems Integration Facility (TSIF) in Alexandria/Arlington, VA — the nation's primary test bed for Transportation Security Equipment (TSE) deployed at airports and transportation hubs nationwide.

Changeis has strong credentials in federal transportation (FAA, Volpe Center), program management, and test & evaluation planning/documentation. However, the DTSS scope is heavily oriented toward physical hardware integration, hands-on equipment testing, laboratory operations, and mass spectrometry/detection systems — disciplines where Changeis has no documented past performance in the BD-RAG corpus. The BD-RAG returned a best composite score of 0.32 (FAA CLMRS), well below the STRONG threshold of 0.65. All matches are FAA IT systems support with incidental T&E documentation components.

Recommended action: Pursue with Partner. Changeis should identify a prime with direct DHS/TSA DT&E heritage (Battelle, SAIC, Leidos, Parsons, or a mid-tier testing specialist) and position as a subcontractor for program management, technical writing, data analysis, and stakeholder coordination services — areas of clear Changeis strength that will be required on any multi-year DTSS contract.
