Data Gaps & Assumptions
Gap 1 — SIR PDF Not Accessible: The ASH-IV SIR attachment's signed URL expired before download. Education/experience requirements were inferred from LCAT naming conventions and the Guidehouse email description. Recommend Varun share the SIR PDF directly for precise edu/exp qualification mapping.
Gap 2 — No Graphic Design LCAT in eFAST: eFAST (PA-26-035) does not include a Web/Graphic Designer labor category. The closest proxy (Int II Technical Writer, avg $97.05) is an imperfect match. Changeis would need to justify this mapping or decline the role.
Gap 3 — eFAST Rates Are PY17 Averages (Market Data): The eFAST rates used are PY17 market averages from the Changeis rate file, not Changeis's actual proposed/negotiated rates. Actual bid rates may differ. Ceiling prices confirm all ASH targets are contractually coverable.
Assumption — On-Site Premium: All roles are on-site at FAA. The eFAST comparison uses the offsite/average rate. Onsite eFAST averages tend to be ~5–10% higher, which slightly narrows but does not eliminate margin headroom.
Assumption — Low Code = Computer Scientist: "Low Code Senior Developer" is mapped to Senior I Computer Scientist/Systems Analyst as the closest eFAST analog. No "Low Code Developer" LCAT exists in eFAST; this is a platform-specific specialty that may warrant a hybrid or custom LCAT justification.
