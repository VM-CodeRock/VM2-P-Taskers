Changeis should brief legal on Perplexity Computer's web-browsing capabilities vs. any pending federal engagements. (Let's Data Science / ACLU)
5
GAAIA Enterprise Deployer Analysis: What Changeis Actually Faces. JD Supra's June 11 deep dive clarifies that GAAIA's mandatory obligations target only $500M+ revenue frontier AI developers — Changeis is almost certainly an AI deployer, not a covered frontier developer. However, three provisions apply broadly: (a) the WARN Act amendment requires additional disclosure when AI is a "substantial factor" in qualifying mass layoffs; (b) doubled fraud penalties for mail/wire/bank fraud apply to any company, AI-related or not; and (c) state deployment law complexity (privacy, employment, contracts) is explicitly not preempted by GAAIA. The stakeholder comment email is now public: GAAIA@mail.house.gov. (JD Supra)
