Executive Summary

SAM.gov issued an update notification for SSD-RFI-1 — the FAA's "Request for Information for Innovative Solutions for FAA Application and Database Modernization" — indicating that Patriot Allied Solutions LLC has been added to the notice. This is a routine interest-list registration event: a vendor has self-identified interest in the RFI on SAM.gov. The underlying RFI (solicitation number SSD-RFI-1, NAICS 541512) had a response deadline of January 7, 2026, and has since evolved into the active ATLAS Challenge Based Acquisition (solicitation ATLAS-1), which is already in Phase 2 with six downselected offerors. Patriot Allied Solutions is a small SDVOSB with no visible federal award history and no apparent FAA work; their addition is not competitively material. No action is required on this notification, but it confirms that smaller firms continue to monitor the FAA modernization space even after the active procurement has moved past the RFI stage.

The Update — What Changed
SAM.gov event: "PATRIOT ALLIED SOLUTIONS LLC added to notice" on SSD-RFI-1 (Special Notice).
  • What "added to notice" means: In SAM.gov terminology, a vendor adding themselves to a notice means they clicked "Follow" or registered interest in the opportunity. It generates a notification email to other followers. It does not indicate a response was submitted, a team was formed, or any competitive action was taken.
  • Timing: The RFI response deadline was January 7, 2026 — nearly five months ago. This interest registration is retrospective and has no procurement effect.
  • ATLAS context: The FAA formally superseded SSD-RFI-1 with the ATLAS-1 solicitation on February 17, 2026. Phase 1 closed March 13, 2026. The FAA downselected to six firms for Phase 2 on April 16, 2026. SSD-RFI-1 is a closed precursor record.
