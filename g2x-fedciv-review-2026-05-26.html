CBP BEAGLE SAILS Recompete — Market Research Launched High Signal

$150M–$450M estimated May 21, 2026 Opportunity — Sources Sought

CBP's Office of Information and Technology (OIT) issued a Sources Sought notice (ID: CBP20153274) for the recompete of the BEAGLE contract — Border Enforcement Applications for Government Leading-Edge: Systems & Applications Integration & Lifecycle Support (SAILS). CBP intends to award a single-award BPA under FAR Part 8.4, with responses from prospective prime offerors due by June 1, 2026.

Scope encompasses application development, 24×7 operations and maintenance, legacy modernization using AI-driven solutions, cloud migration, DevSecOps, data analytics, cybersecurity audits, and enterprise application management. The tech stack spans legacy COBOL alongside modern .NET, Java, Python, JavaScript, and cloud platforms, as well as Salesforce, SAP Business Objects, and ServiceNow. Place of performance: Ashburn, VA.

Changeis BD Relevance
CBP/DHS is a tier-1 Changeis target account. This single-award BPA is one of the largest CBP IT recompetes in the market right now. The requirement's emphasis on agile, modernization, legacy systems, and data analytics aligns squarely with Changeis technical capabilities. June 1 prime-intent deadline means capture strategy must be initiated immediately. Consider teaming discussions with established CBP incumbents or large-prime leads.
