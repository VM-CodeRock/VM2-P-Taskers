Omi AI Wearable — Product Research & Competitive Landscape

Created: 2026-05-25 · Source: omi.me/pages/product · Category: AI wearable / always-on voice capture pendant

Executive Summary

  • Product: Omi DEV KIT 2 — a quarter-sized AI pendant (2.5 cm × 1.5 cm, 150 mAh, 10–14 hr battery) that transcribes everything you say and hear, then auto-generates summaries, action items, and a searchable "memory" graph.
  • Pricing: $89 one-time + optional $16/mo Unlimited cloud transcription. Free tier ships with 1,200 cloud minutes/month and unlimited on-device transcription.
  • Positioning: The open-source, developer-friendly entry in the AI-wearable category — cheapest hardware, lowest subscription, only player with open hardware + open software, MCP server, and an App Store where third parties ship paid apps.
  • Privacy posture: SOC 2 + HIPAA, TLS in transit, AES-256-GCM at rest, no training on user data, optional fully-local mode. Strong for a regulated user (legal, healthcare, federal-adjacent).
  • Direct competitors: Limitless Pendant (~$99 + $19–29/mo), Plaud NotePin (~$159), Bee Pioneer Edition (~$49 + $19/mo), Humane Ai Pin (effectively sunset), and screen-based alternative Screenpipe.
  • Bottom line: Omi is the value + hackability play. Limitless wins on enterprise polish and 100-hour battery. Plaud wins on call-recording / MagSafe form factor. Bee wins on price for casual lifelogging.

2. Privacy & data control (best → weakest)

  1. Omi — open source, local mode, SOC 2 + HIPAA, AES-256-GCM, no training on user data, full export.
  2. Screenpipe — fully local, no cloud, but software-only (no pendant).
  3. Plaud — 64 GB local buffer, but cloud-dependent for AI features.
  4. Limitless — cloud-only processing; offers Consent Mode for in-meeting privacy.
  5. Bee — cloud-dependent, owned by Amazon (data-handling questions remain).

3. Developer / extensibility

  • Omi wins decisively — open hardware, open software, MCP server, public API, no-code app builder, App Store revenue share. Best fit for AI tinkerers
  • Limitless has a Mac desktop app and integrations (Zoom, Slack, Google Meet) but is closed-source.
  • Plaud, Bee, Humane are closed ecosystems.

4. Enterprise / meeting use

  • Limitless wins for executives — 100-hour battery, best speaker ID, captures desktop Zoom/Teams audio alongside in-room audio, organized "nested bullet" meeting summaries ([Forbes Vetted](https://www.forbes.com/sites/forbes-personal-shopper/article/best-ai-wearables/)).
  • Omi is a close second on capability, much cheaper, and the only one that gives you the raw API to integrate with internal CRM / proposal tools.

Strategic Read — Relevance to Changeis / Federal BD Workflows

For a BD workflow heavy on meetings, capture-the-conversation interviews, RFI debriefs, and capture-management calls, the choice narrows to Omi or Limitless. Both fit, but they optimize for different things.
  • Pick Omi if the priority is integrating capture into a custom pipeline (RAG over conversations, MCP into Claude / Perplexity, custom summary templates aligned to Changeis capture-management format). Open API + open hardware also lets you keep transcripts inside your own infrastructure for SOC 2 / FedRAMP-adjacent scenarios.
  • Pick Limitless if the priority is "best-in-class, low-touch executive meeting recorder" with strong speaker ID, 100-hour battery, and Zoom/Teams desktop sync that Just Works for an exec who does not want to tinker.
  • Avoid Bee for federal/BD use — Amazon ownership creates data-handling questions on client-privileged conversations.
  • Watch: Plaud's MagSafe NotePin variant is interesting for capturing phone calls (vibration mic) — a real gap in the others.
  • Compliance note for all: Maryland is a two-party consent state; any of these devices used in meetings with external parties needs explicit recording consent. Omi and Limitless both build "consent" workflows into the product.
