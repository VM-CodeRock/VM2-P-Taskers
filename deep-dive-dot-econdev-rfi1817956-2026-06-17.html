"MRA" Acronym Resolution
Best Determination — High Confidence
MRA = Market Research As a Service (MRAS) — the GSA program that conducts FAR Part 10 market research on behalf of federal customer agencies using GSA eBuy as the posting platform.

The title convention on GSA eBuy RFIs issued through the MRAS program consistently appends "-MRA" to the agency-assigned title. According to the GSA MRAS Industry FAQ: "MRAS is the program office within GSA that works with the customer agency to issue the RFI on their behalf. MRAS issues the RFI." All postings are approved by the customer agency before publication.

This means the title structure is: [Customer Agency] – [Requirement Title] – MRA. So for this posting:

  • Customer Agency: DOT (Department of Transportation)
  • Requirement: Economic Development Oversight Support
  • MRA: Market Research As a Service facilitation marker

Eliminated alternatives:

  • MARAD (Maritime Administration): MARAD is a DOT sub-agency, not an acronym used as a suffix in eBuy titles. MARAD would appear in the agency field, not the title suffix. However, MARAD remains a strong candidate as the requirement owner within DOT.
  • Modal Risk Assessment: No established DOT acronym or contracting usage found.
  • Mission Review Assessment: Informal usage in DOT OIG context only; not a contracting artifact.
