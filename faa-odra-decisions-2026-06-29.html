The FAA ODRA is the sole forum for FAA bid protests under 14 CFR Part 17; it does not share jurisdiction with GAO or COFC. FASA task order protest bars do not apply to FAA.

Standing 2026 Precedent — Protest of Cavan Solutions (2026 WL 1284037)

Precedent Status — Remains Controlling; No Subsequent Orders

The Protest of Cavan Solutions, 2026 WL 1284037 (O.D.R.A. 2026), remains the only confirmed 2026 ODRA final order. Published to legal databases May 15, 2026 by Bradley Arant Boult Cummings via JD Supra, the decision involves the FAA SETIS program (IDIQ, ~$23.8M task order). Key holdings remain in full force:

  • No-protest clauses in FAA IDIQ task orders are unenforceable on three independent statutory grounds: (1) 49 U.S.C. § 40110(d)(4) mandates ODRA adjudication; (2) FASA task order protest bars do not apply to FAA (49 U.S.C. § 40110(d)(2)); (3) 5 U.S.C. § 575(a)(3) prohibits contractual arbitration waivers.
  • OCI is "unmitigable" where former FAA employees are directly involved in related competitive evaluations. Corrective action standard: voluntary disclosure, investigation, task order termination.
  • Transparency rule: ODRA committed to publishing decisions over agency objection. Expect more precedents to surface as 2026 docket items clear publication lag.
  • AMS 2016 memorandum approving no-protest clause found misleading by ODRA; current AMS Policy 3.9.3 is weaker than the 2016 Huerta-approved version and does not rehabilitate the clause.

Sources: JD Supra (May 15, 2026) · National Law Review (May 15, 2026) · LinkedIn / Bradley Law Roundup (Jun 3, 2026).

Revolutionary FAR Overhaul — Proposed Part 33 Agency-Level Protest Enhancement

The FAR Council published four proposed rules on June 23, 2026 covering 20 FAR parts as part of the "Revolutionary FAR Overhaul" (EO 14275). Proposed FAR Part 33 would shift primary protest authority to agencies, require contracting officers to report protests to heads of contracting activities, and authorize agencies to provide protesters with redacted source selection decisions. For FAA contractors, this structurally mirrors the existing ODRA model — FAA already adjudicates protests internally rather than at GAO. Comments due July 23, 2026. The expanded agency-level protest visibility may increase protest frequency on FAA task orders; Changeis should ensure source selection documentation is defensible on ATEPS-II awards.
