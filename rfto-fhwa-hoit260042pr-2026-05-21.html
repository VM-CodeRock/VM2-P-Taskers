FHWA Contract Specialist Catherine Shade (IT Acquisition Center of Excellence) issued RFTO HOIT260042PR on May 21, 2026 under the IPSS3 BPA. The task order funds the ITS JPO Enterprise Program Management Office (EPMO) HUB — a custom web application for the ITS Joint Program Office's research portfolio management — and the Financial Management System (FMS) for approximately 120 external users. The work is Firm-Fixed Price, 12-month base + 2 option years (36 months total). Six labor categories are required, five of which are designated Key Personnel, all with 10+ year software engineering minimums (Angular, C#, .NET, SQL stack).

Changeis's RAG portfolio scan returned 10 relevant matches. The strongest alignment is the corporate capabilities statement (ITS Standards Program, FHWA Office of Operations Connected Vehicle Deployment), prior FHWA HOP task order (V3362009, Contract 6913G620F400102), and DOT financial toolset work on FAA eFAST BAFM III. The PMO/program management wrapper around the HUB (Task 1 — contract management, PMP, monthly reporting, schedule management) maps directly to Changeis competencies. The software development core (Tasks 2–4, Angular/.NET/SQL engineering) requires a specialist sub-contractor.
