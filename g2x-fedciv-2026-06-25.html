2 FedCiv Article Roundup — June 24–25, 2026

Headlines from Nextgov/FCW, MeriTalk, GovConWire, OrangeSlices, and ExecutiveBiz. Sources linked where available.

NASA / Civilian Agencies Jun 23–24, 2026 GWAC / SEWP VI / $60B
NASA Names 2,115 Winners for SEWP VI — Three Categories, $60B Ceiling, Ordering Opens Nov. 1
NASA completed initial awards under SEWP VI with 2,115 contract awards across three categories: Category A (364 awards — IT Solutions), Category B (692 awards — Enterprise-Wide IT Services), and Category C (1,059 awards — IT Mission-Based Services). Approximately 1,490 unique firms are represented; companies could win in multiple categories. The $60B vehicle opens for ordering November 1, 2026, replacing SEWP V which sunsets September 30 (with extension to April 2027 if needed). The vehicle operates for 10 years through October 31, 2036.
White House / DOD / DOE Jun 25, 2026 Quantum Computing / Executive Orders
Trump Signs Two Executive Orders to Prepare the U.S. for a Quantum Future; DOE Unveils Quantum Genesis Plan
President Trump signed two long-awaited executive orders directing federal agencies to protect current IT infrastructure against fault-tolerant quantum computers and accelerating U.S. quantum research via DOE-hosted quantum computer development. DOE's "Quantum Genesis" mission has already attracted 5,000 research proposals and will focus on fusion energy and healthcare breakthroughs. First Genesis mission awards are expected July 22. Senate is also considering quantum initiative reauthorization as part of NDAA.
NASA Jun 25, 2026 People / Leadership
Sean Gallagher Selected as Permanent NASA CIO, Effective Immediately
NASA named Sean Gallagher as its permanent Chief Information Officer, effective immediately. Gallagher had been serving as acting CIO since January 2026. The appointment comes as NASA navigates the SEWP VI transition, multiple major IT procurements, and the DOE Quantum Genesis collaboration — all requiring sustained CIO-level leadership continuity.
FAR / GSA / Acquisition Policy Jun 24–25, 2026 Acquisition Reform / Rulemaking
Revolutionary FAR Overhaul Moves to Formal Rulemaking — Four Proposed Rules Covering 13 FAR Parts Published
The GSA-led FAR overhaul published its first batch of four proposed rules covering 13 Federal Acquisition Regulation parts. Small business rules and additional commercial item updates are forthcoming. This is the most significant FAR restructuring in decades and will directly affect how civilian agencies procure IT and professional services. Contractors should monitor for changes to simplified acquisition thresholds, commercial item definitions, and small business set-aside structures.
DHS / AWS Jun 12–24, 2026 Cloud / Cumulus / $2.56B
DHS Awards $2.56B to AWS as First Cumulus Cloud Contract; Microsoft, Google, Oracle Awards Expected Q3 FY26
DHS awarded Amazon Web Services a $2.56B single-award IDIQ (contract 70RTAC26D00000004) as the first Cumulus cloud services contract — covering IaaS, PaaS, SaaS, marketplace, and training services department-wide. Separate contracts for Microsoft, Google Cloud, and Oracle are expected in Q3 FY26. DHS estimates $142M in first-year savings from Cumulus centralization. The award covers all 22 DHS components including CBP, USCIS, TSA, ICE, FEMA, and USSS.
NIH / NITAAC Jun 9–24, 2026 GWAC Sunset / CIO-SP3 / Vehicle Transition
NIH NITAAC Formally Announces Sunset of CIO-SP3, CIO-SP3 SB, and CIO-CS — Final Orders Due Oct. 29, 2026
NIH's NITAAC contracting office confirmed the sunset of all three GWACs (CIO-SP3, CIO-SP3 Small Business, CIO-CS) effective October 29, 2026. New orders placed after June 8 may not extend beyond December 31, 2028. NITAAC will cease all program functions after 2028. Future requirements will transition to GSA's portfolio (primarily SEWP VI and OASIS+). This creates immediate urgency for any contractor whose NIH, HHS, or federal pipeline relies on CIO-SP3 as the ordering vehicle.
DOT / FAA Jun 23–25, 2026 AI / Contract Award / $875M
FAA Awards Air Space Intelligence $875M to Modernize Air Traffic Control System with Software and AI (Confirmed)
Nextgov confirmed the FAA's $875M contract with Air Space Intelligence for air traffic control modernization using software and AI. The system will serve as "the new technological backbone" of a modernized ATC System Command Center, covering FMDS and SMART platforms. DOT is also pursuing post-quantum cryptography RFI responses for FAA systems and a broader WCF IT transformation requiring a PMO/change management prime — a relevant opportunity for Changeis 541611.
TSA / DOT Jun 24, 2026 IT Support / $100M+ / RFP Watch
TSA Plans Potential $100M FOCUS Contract for Enterprise-Wide IT Support & Help Desk Services
The Transportation Security Administration announced a contract opportunity valued at more than $100M for enterprise-wide IT support and help desk services under the FOCUS vehicle. TSA is a DHS component buyer and a priority agency for IT support contracts. Monitor SAM.gov for solicitation release. NAICS 541512 alignment. TSA has been active on IT modernization following its Microsoft Software Licenses and Support task order awarded to Dell ($59M) in June.
GSA / Agencies Jun 24–25, 2026 AI Platform / USAi Expansion
GSA to Add 16 More Agencies to USAi Federal AI Testing Platform by End of 2026; Cost-Recovery Model Starting FY27
GSA Deputy Administrator Michael Lynch confirmed plans to onboard 16 additional federal agencies to USAi (now 25+ participants), the government's shared AI evaluation platform offering access to OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta, Gemini, Microsoft, xAI, and Amazon models. GSA will transition USAi to a cost-recovery model in FY27. Separately, GSA is also preparing an AI acquisition reform rule with a preference for fixed-price AI contracts. The OneGov/Snowflake deal (up to 50% compute discounts) announced May 21 continues to drive civilian cloud adoption.
DOE / Quantum Jun 24–25, 2026 Quantum Computing / Emerging Tech
DOE Unveils Plan for Scientifically-Relevant Fault-Tolerant Quantum Computer via Quantum Genesis Mission
DOE's Quantum Genesis mission will build and host a fault-tolerant quantum computer for scientific research, as directed under one of Trump's two new quantum executive orders. The Argonne National Laboratory AI inference service for researchers is now live, supporting AI model experimentation on high-performance computing. First Genesis mission awards are expected July 22, 2026. For contractors, quantum-adjacent advisory, infrastructure, and integration services at DOE labs represent a medium-term pipeline.
Commerce / DOC Jun 17–25, 2026 CHIPS Act / AI / $500M
Commerce Awards SandboxAQ $500M CHIPS Act R&D Contract for AI-Driven Semiconductor Materials Discovery
The Department of Commerce signed a definitive $500M CHIPS Act agreement with SandboxAQ (Alphabet spinoff, backed by Nvidia) to develop AI-driven materials discovery for domestic semiconductor manufacturing. SandboxAQ's Large Quantitative Models (physics-based AI) will target PFAS-free chemicals, catalysts, rare earth-free magnets, and batteries for semiconductor fabs. Commerce receives a minority, non-voting equity stake. This award signals Commerce's willingness to make major direct technology investments and its appetite for AI-driven R&D.
CISA / Cybersecurity Jun 24–25, 2026 Cybersecurity / CDM / AI Models
CISA Completes CDM Endpoint Detection & Response Deployment Across Federal Agencies; Orders New Vulnerability Management Tiered Deadlines
CISA CDM program manager Matt House confirmed the agency completed its effort to help federal agencies deploy endpoint detection and response (EDR) tools across the CDM program — a major cybersecurity milestone. Separately, CISA issued a new binding operational directive setting tiered remediation timelines for agencies including a 3-day deadline for the highest-risk vulnerabilities. CISA is also accelerating hiring with 329 new FTEs and 180 offers expected by month-end following the AI executive order wave.
HHS / CMS Jun 12–24, 2026 Health IT / Data Consolidation / $6B Savings
CMS Consolidates Eight Provider Directories into Single National Directory, Projects $6B Annual Savings
CMS consolidated eight separate provider directories into a single national directory, with agency officials estimating $6B annually in eliminated administrative costs from eliminating redundant provider data verification across systems. This initiative aligns with DOGE efficiency priorities and demonstrates CMS's data modernization direction. For IT contractors, the single-directory platform represents an integration and data quality services opportunity under 541511/541512.
OPM / Oracle Jun 10–24, 2026 HR Modernization / $396M / Cloud
OPM Awards Oracle $395.8M 10-Year Contract to Overhaul Federal HR Systems (Federal HR 2.0 — 119 Legacy Systems Replaced)
OPM awarded Oracle a $395.8M, 10-year contract to replace 119 legacy HR systems serving approximately 2 million federal civilian employees with a single cloud-based Core HCM platform. Designated "Federal HR 2.0," the program targets core implementation in fall 2026 and agency onboarding throughout FY27. The award came via a sole-source justification citing Oracle's unique cloud HCM FedRAMP posture. OPM also expanded its Tech Force initiative with 9 new industry partners this week.
DOD / NSA / CISA Jun 25, 2026 AI Models / Cybersecurity / Mythos 5
NSA Mythos 5 Access Disrupted by Anthropic Supply Chain Dispute; CISA Gains Full Mythos Preview Access
Parts of NSA lost access to Anthropic's Mythos 5 model amid a supply chain dispute between Anthropic and government cloud providers — a sign of growing AI model supply chain risk in national security contexts. CISA, meanwhile, received full Mythos Preview access about a week ago, though the White House has not yet set clear parameters for how the agency should use the model. The Five Eyes alliance is warning that frontier AI could soon accelerate both cyberattacks and cyber defense simultaneously.
