Scope, Deliverables & Requirements

Mission Context

GCERC is a federal council managing BP oil spill restoration funds under the RESTORE Act. This Data Analyst supports the GCERC Director of Ecosystem Restoration Programs with programmatic tracking, records management, document finalization, and SharePoint data QA for the COAST site. The work is primarily administrative/program-management in character — not advanced data science. Think "senior program coordinator" with data analyst title.

Performance Objectives

  • Objective 1: Data Entry and Record Management Support — records tracking spreadsheet, field site visit tracking, liaison with records management contractor
  • Objective 2: Development and Finalization of Programmatic Documents — SOPs, status reports, staff assignment tracking, programmatic doc support (Word, Google Docs, presentations, figures)
