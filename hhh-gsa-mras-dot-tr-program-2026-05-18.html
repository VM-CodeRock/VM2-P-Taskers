4 — GSA MRAS Context
FACTUAL — Extracted from DOT-Technology-Transfer-Program-MRAS.pdf & dot-tr-GSA-eBuy.pdf

What is MRAS? Market Research as a Service (MRAS) is a GSA program (see www.gsa.gov/mras) through which GSA conducts market research on behalf of federal agencies. GSA issues an RFI survey through the MRAS platform (powered by Qualtrics) to qualified MAS holders. [Source: DOT-Technology-Transfer-Program-MRAS.pdf, p.1]

How MRAS Differs from a Solicitation: An MRAS RFI is not a solicitation and does not result in an award. The Government will not compile a bidders list, nor is any award made from the MRAS survey. The purpose is informational — to identify capable firms, understand market capacity, and inform acquisition planning. However, per GSAR 538.7103-2, MAS holders who respond may be directly invited to future RFQs — making this the de facto on-ramp to competition. [Source: GSA-RFI-DOT-TR.docx; DOT-Technology-Transfer-Program-MRAS.pdf, p.2]

What GSA Wants from Respondents: GSA seeks structured survey responses covering: [Source: DOT-Technology-Transfer-Program-MRAS.pdf, pp.5–10]

  • Company and POC information (email, GSA contract number, UEI, CAGE)
  • Business size and socioeconomic indicators under the relevant GSA contract
  • Technical capability confirmation (5 yes/no questions on capability, experience, past performance)
  • Estimated subcontracting percentage
  • Lead time to performance start
  • Intent to submit a quote/proposal if an RFQ is issued
  • Applicable GSA contracts (MAS, OASIS+) and SINs/NAICS
  • A Capabilities Statement (up to 15 pages, one PDF) with specific past performance and corporate experience data
  • Optional: Capability website URL
