USDOT · DHS · GSA · DOI · Treasury · Commerce · VA
Wednesday, July 15, 2026 — coverage window: past 24-48 hours
1 Agency News, Awards & Policy Shifts
  • GSA / IT

    Empower AI secured the next-generation DIGIT (Digital Innovation for GSA Infrastructure Technologies) task order under the Alliant 2 GWAC, valued at $145.6 million over two years with performance beginning July 1, extending its role as GSA's enterprise IT modernization partner (Business Wire).

  • GSA

    Cherokee Nation Solutions Link was named sole awardee of GSA's Office of Centralized Acquisition Services Modernization Support Services enterprise support BPA, positioning the Cherokee Federal unit as the exclusive vehicle for OCAS acquisition-modernization work (Yahoo Finance / PRNewswire).

  • GSA

    GSA issued a Request for Information seeking industry input on strengthening American-made product procurement through GSA Advantage!, building on FY25 enforcement that blocked 70,915 non-compliant Trade Agreements Act items and removed roughly 39,000 items from Federal Supply Schedule contracts (GSA newsroom).

  • DHS / CISA

    CISA launched the ANCHOR-CI framework to rebuild critical-infrastructure information sharing after a roughly year-long gap; the program runs an initial two-year term renewable in additional two-year increments (Utility Dive, MedRisk).

  • DHS / ICE

    DHS ordered ICE to suspend most vehicle stops nationwide following two fatal shootings of motorists in Biddeford, Maine, and Houston within the past week; agents in both incidents were not wearing body cameras despite a February DHS directive to equip the field with them (Atlanta Journal-Constitution, USA Today).

  • DOI / Commerce

    President Trump signed an executive order cutting nearly 3 million acres from Utah's Bears Ears and Grand Staircase-Escalante national monuments, the administration's second attempt at reducing the sites; Interior and Commerce jointly issued a companion final rule the same week rescinding the regulatory definition of "harm" under the Endangered Species Act, removing habitat modification as a standalone basis for unlawful "take" (Reuters, White House fact sheet, Save the Manatee Club).

  • Treasury

    Treasury reported June's federal deficit swelled to roughly $120 billion, driven in part by tariff refunds triggered by a Supreme Court ruling, complicating near-term fiscal projections (Ground News).

  • VA / Oversight

    House Republicans demanded an investigation into a major veterans' service organization tied to a VA benefits bill dispute; VA has not yet said whether it will open a formal inquiry (Newsweek).

  • VA

    VA's Captain James A. Lovell Federal Health Care Center began deploying VR-based BraveMind exposure therapy for post-9/11 veterans with PTS, donated by nonprofit SoldierStrong, extending a device rollout already active at other VA facilities (GlobeNewswire / Business Insider).

  • USDOT / Senate Commerce

    The Senate Commerce Committee scheduled a July 15 markup covering DOT-related nominations, including Edward Eppler for DOT Chief Financial Officer and Karen Jean Hedlund for the Surface Transportation Board (Senate Commerce Committee).

2 Bid Protests & GAO/OIG Activity
  • GAO / VA

    GAO sustained Veterans Management Services' protest of a VA Veterans Health Administration award, finding the agency improperly applied an undisclosed three-year cutoff to exclude four highly rated CPARS past-performance records, and ordered a new evaluation and source-selection decision (National Law Review, GovConFeed).

  • FAR Council

    The FAR Council proposed revisions to FAR Part 33 making agency-level bid protests more attractive by requiring agencies to provide protesters a redacted Source Selection Decision Document upon independent review; comments on the civilian-and-defense-wide change are due July 23 (Greenberg Traurig).

  • GAO / NASA SEWP VI

    NASA cleared the final substantive obstacle on its roughly $60 billion SEWP VI GWAC after GAO denied the last of 12 protests on July 2; the only outstanding action is a reconsideration request GAO is expected to deny (Orange Slices).

  • Federal Circuit

    In Utech, Inc. v. United States (June 24), the Federal Circuit clarified that the proposal-submission deadline, not contract award, is the operative "close of bidding" for Blue & Gold waiver purposes — a nonprecedential but influential signal for civilian-agency pre-award protest timing (Crowell & Moring).

3 OMB Uniform Grants Regulation — Post-Comment Tracker
  • OMB / 2 CFR Part 200

    The 45-day comment window on OMB's proposed Uniform Grants Regulation rewrite of 2 CFR Part 200 (docket OMB-2026-0034) closed at 11:59 p.m. ET July 13; submissions approached nearly 100,000 comments, with counties, universities, and nonprofits filing objections to political-appointee pre-issuance review, expanded termination-for-convenience authority, and new E-Verify/domestic-records-storage mandates (University of Washington Federal Relations, Eos, Granted AI).

  • OMB / Grants

    OMB is now reviewing comments ahead of a targeted October 1 final-rule effective date for FY2027 awards; NACo, AAU, and the National Low Income Housing Coalition were among groups urging Congress to use appropriations authority to block or delay implementation before OMB issues a final rule (National Association of Counties, NLIHC).
