Active Since Jun 30

Section 805 (FY2024 NDAA) Entity Ban — Day 2 Active (as of Jun 30, 2026): The Department of Defense is prohibited from entering into, renewing, or extending contracts with any entity on the Section 1260H List of Chinese Military Companies or entities subject to their control. DoD published the updated 1260H List on June 8, 2026, designating 188 Chinese companies. Key designations relevant to aerospace/aviation supply chains: AVIC subsidiaries (Align Aerospace LLC, Avicopter PLC, Cirrus Design Corporation / Cirrus Aircraft, Continental Aerospace Technologies), Alibaba, Baidu, BYD, NIO, Unitree Robotics, and WuXi AppTec. The Product Ban (Section 805) takes effect June 30, 2027. Changeis BD and legal teams must audit all FAA/DoD teaming and subcontract arrangements for 1260H-listed company supply chain exposure before any active proposal submission. Sources: WilmerHale / TechTimes / DoD 1260H List PDF.
