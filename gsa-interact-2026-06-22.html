Policy / FAR Watch
FAR 52.222-90 DEI Clause — Retroactive to All MAS Orders; July 24 Compliance Deadline for Existing Contracts
Effective June 4, 2026 (MAS Refresh 32) | Source: Gormley Group, Holland & Knight

FAR 52.222-90 (EO 14398, March 26, 2026) is now incorporated into all MAS contracts via Refresh 32 and applies retroactively to all existing and future orders. The clause prohibits racially discriminatory DEI activities — defined as disparate treatment in recruitment, employment, contracting, and resource allocation based on race or ethnicity. For non-MAS GSA contracts, the July 24, 2026 deadline applies for existing contract modification. Key elements: (1) Certify no racially discriminatory DEI activities; (2) Provide compliance information on request; (3) Report subcontractor violations; (4) No retaliation against employees reporting violations.

Changeis Impact: Review all current DEI program descriptions, marketing materials, and HR policies. Ensure no language could be interpreted as racially discriminatory. Accept Refresh 32 mass mod immediately to start the 30-day compliance period. Consult counsel if current programs include any race-based preferences in hiring, vendor selection, or resource allocation.

GSAR 552.239-7001 American AI Clause — Deferred from Refresh 32; Timing Now Uncertain
Status as of June 22, 2026 | Source: Gormley Group, Winvale

The American AI clause (GSAR 552.239-7001) was again deferred — not included in Refresh 32 despite earlier expectations. GSA stated it is "finalizing" the clause but no new timeline has been provided. Requirements remain expected to include: use of "American AI Systems" only; 30-day disclosure of all AI systems post-award; government ownership of custom developments; prohibition on using government data to train models; 72-hour CISA incident reporting; 30-day notice before changing AI Service Provider.

Changeis Impact: Preparation work from prior reviews remains valid. Use this additional lead time to complete the AI system inventory. The delay does not reduce eventual compliance burden — it gives Changeis a window to remediate gaps before the clock starts. Maintain watch on Interact for Refresh 33 advance notice.
