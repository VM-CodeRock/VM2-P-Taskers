Strategic Notes — G.8.3 Clause Audit & Cavan Solutions ODRA Ruling
Audit Due Saturday June 13: The Clause G.8.3 audit was scheduled based on the Protest of Cavan Solutions (2026 WL 1284037) ODRA ruling. This audit directly affects Changeis’s ATEPS-II obligations and competitive exposure. Three days remain.

The FAA ODRA’s Protest of Cavan Solutions, issued in Q1–Q2 2026 and published to legal databases around May 15, 2026, struck down SETIS contract clause G.8.3 on three independent grounds. G.8.3 purported to make task order awards “final and conclusive” and non-protestable for awards below $25M. The ODRA held the clause void based on: (1) FAA AMS and ODRA jurisdictional authority; (2) statutory conflict with the procurement integrity framework; (3) policy grounds. The ruling’s logic applies beyond SETIS to all FAA IDIQs, including ATEPS-II.

Changeis-specific exposure: Changeis holds ATEPS-II Award 693KA925D00004 ($114.7M, Dec 2024–Dec 2034). Any task order solicitation clause attempting to bar protests is now presumptively void. This creates:

  • Risk: Competitors can now protest ATEPS-II task order awards to Changeis regardless of any no-protest clause. Expected surge in protests across FAA IDIQ vehicles in the 12–18 months following the ruling.
  • Opportunity: Changeis can protest task order awards it believes were improperly evaluated, including any awards where it was displaced by a competitor with unmitigated OCI.
  • OCI screening: The Cavan Solutions case centered on former FAA employees joining AS&T (the displaced awardee). The ODRA found an “unmitigable, unavoidable OCI.” Changeis’s deep FAA ATO PMO relationships make proactive OCI mitigation documentation essential.
