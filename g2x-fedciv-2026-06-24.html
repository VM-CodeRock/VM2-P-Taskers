DOT / FAA Jun 23, 2026 AI / Contract Award / $875M
Air Space Intelligence Wins $875M FAA Contract to Modernize National Airspace System with AI
Air Space Intelligence was awarded an $875M, 12-year FAA contract to modernize the National Airspace System using software and AI. The deal covers deployment of FMDS — which will anchor the FAA Air Traffic Control System Command Center — and SMART, which manages traffic demand before flights take off. This is the largest FAA IT modernization award of FY26 and signals a sustained pipeline of AI/software support work at DOT.
NASA / Civilian Agencies Jun 22–23, 2026 Contract Vehicle / SEWP VI
NASA Awards SEWP VI IT Contract Vehicle to 364 Firms; Three $20B Ceilings, 10-Year Ordering Period
NASA announced SEWP VI awards to 364 initial awardees including Booz Allen, CACI, Carahsoft, Chenega, ECS Federal, IBM, Leidos, Parsons, and Peraton. Each of three categories carries a $20B maximum ceiling; ordering runs November 1, 2026 through October 31, 2036. SEWP VI will be a primary civilian IT procurement vehicle for the next decade, affecting virtually every federal civilian agency's technology buy.
GSA Jun 23–24, 2026 Cloud / OneGov / Snowflake
GSA Inks OneGov Agreement with Snowflake; Agencies Eligible for Up to 50% Compute Cost Discounts
GSA's OneGov program reached a new agreement with Snowflake, enabling participating federal agencies to access discounts of up to 50% on compute consumption as usage scales. GSA is also preparing an AI-specific acquisition reform rule for the next few weeks, with a preference for fixed-price models. These moves position GSA as the civilian cloud and AI procurement anchor for FY27.
