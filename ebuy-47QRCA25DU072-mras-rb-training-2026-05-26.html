A. Executive Summary

The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) has issued a Market Research Action (MRA) request via GSA eBuy (RFI1813561, under solicitation reference 47QRCA25DU072) for a multi-year training services contract to support three records-based data collection systems: the Fatality Analysis Reporting System (FARS), the Crash Report Sampling System (CRSS), and the Non-Traffic Surveillance (NTS) system.

This is a highly specialized, mission-critical contract requiring deep domain expertise in NHTSA data systems that Changeis does not currently demonstrate through BD-RAG matched past performance. The BD-RAG returned a LOW composite score (0.3228 on a 0–1 scale), with all top matches being adjacent DOT data quality work (FMCSA/Volpe) rather than direct NHTSA FARS/CRSS training. This gap is material.

Key Decision: This opportunity is a NO-BID as a prime. However, the DOT/NHTSA relationship via existing Volpe-adjacent work (V3132249, V3132355) and Changeis’s WOSB/EDWOSB status creates a meaningful teaming angle. If an incumbent NHTSA training firm (e.g., the current MRAS contractor) is willing to take Changeis as a small business subcontractor or team partner, a GO decision could be revisited. Deadline for teaming outreach: May 30, 2026 (5 days before bid close).
