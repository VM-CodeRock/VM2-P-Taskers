Section A — Standard Operating Procedure

A1. Membership Chair Charter & Authority

The Membership Chair is a board-level officer role with a one-year term (YPO fiscal year: July 1 – June 30, with summer break July 1–August 30). The Chair holds delegated authority from the Chapter Board to:

  • Accept or reject candidates for formal pipeline entry (GO/NO-GO at initial vet)
  • Constitute and direct the Membership Committee (typically 6–10 members)
  • Conduct or delegate all vetting interviews
  • Sign membership recommendation memos to the board
  • Manage YPO Connect chapter portal access and candidate records
  • Represent the chapter on YPO Global membership matters (coordinate with YPO Join Manager Julianna Lee)
  • Set chapter-specific membership requirements above YPO Global baseline

The Vice Chair (currently being recruited — Kiely Wilkerson was in discussion as of April 2026) serves as a true co-pilot: interchangeable at all functions, primary backup for panel interviews, and successor-designate for the following year.

YPO DC Chapter Requirements (above Global baseline):
Global: Under 45 · CEO/President/equivalent · 50+ FTE or $2.5M+ employee comp · $15M+ revenue ($12M agency, $300M financial institution AUM).
YPO DC chapter is considering raising requirements above YPO Global baseline to maintain exclusivity. US Capital chapter (75 members, "uber exclusive") is the benchmark. Any requirement changes must be approved by the full board.

Stage 1

Sponsorship — Candidate Identification

Owner: Sponsor + Chair Duration: Ongoing / no fixed timeline Deliverable: Warm intro email to Chair

A current YPO DC member (the "Sponsor") introduces a candidate to the Chair or Vice Chair. The sponsor vouches for the candidate's character, YPO fit, and approximate eligibility. Introductions are typically CC'd to Donna Shea (donna.shea@changeis.com) for scheduling.

  • Gate to Stage 2: Chair receives intro email; schedules initial meeting within 2 weeks
  • Common failure modes: Sponsor introduces without checking age/revenue eligibility first; no follow-through on scheduling
  • Chair action: Reply within 48 hours using Sponsor Acknowledgement template (Section D); direct Donna to schedule
Stage 2

Initial Vet — 1:1 Meeting with Chair/Vice Chair

Owner: Chair (or Vice Chair) Duration: 30–60 min meeting; GO/NO-GO within 7 days post-meeting Deliverable: Meeting notes + GO/NO-GO decision logged in tracker

The Chair conducts a structured introductory meeting with the prospective member. Purpose: learn about their business and interests; share what YPO DC offers; assess basic fit (character, engagement orientation, YPO eligibility). This is not the formal vetting — it is a mutual discovery conversation.

  • Key questions to cover: Business model and scale; age; leadership role; motivation for YPO; prior peer group experience; any known concerns
  • Gate to Stage 3: Chair issues GO (enter pipeline) or NO-GO (decline gracefully). GO is logged in ypo_tracker/introductions.json and communicated to Eliana Hassen / Jill Goldfine at Redda Group to initiate next steps
  • Common failure modes: Delay in GO/NO-GO decision leaves candidate and sponsor waiting (target: ≤7 days); failing to loop in Redda Group/Jill after GO decision
  • SLA: Husein Sharaf case illustrates the risk — meeting completed April 20 but GO/NO-GO outstanding as of May 27 (37+ days). This is a critical backlog item.
Stage 3

Formal Application + Third-Party Verification

Owner: Candidate (guided by Jill Goldfine / YPO Global) Duration: 1–3 weeks for application; 3rd party verification valid 90 days Deliverable: Application submitted to YPO Global; verification complete; candidate in chapter portal

Candidate submits formal application at ypo.org/become-a-member, selecting Washington DC Chapter. Chair confirms candidate is placed in YPO DC chapter portal on YPO Connect. YPO Global initiates third-party financial verification (independent CPA, attorney, or auditor — not someone who works for the candidate's company).

  • Verification contacts: Must be independent — CPA or outside auditor acceptable (per Julianna Lee guidance). Verifier submits S-document directly to YPO.
  • Gate to Stage 4: Application in system + verification complete (or underway). Note: chapter can begin Stage 4 activities while verification is pending.
  • Common failure modes: Candidate submits to wrong chapter; verifier is internal (not independent); verification expires (90-day window)
  • Chair action: Confirm receipt of application in portal; coordinate with Julianna Lee (npremkumar1@hotmail.com or YPO Join line) on verification status
Stage 4

Champion Meeting + Two Chapter Learning Events + Prospective Member Dinner

Owner: Membership Committee Champion (assigned by Chair) + Jill Goldfine (event invitations) Duration: 4–8 weeks depending on event calendar Deliverable: Champion meeting completed; 2 Learning Events attended; PM Luncheon/Dinner attended

Once in formal pipeline, Chair assigns a Membership Committee Champion — a current YPO DC member who serves as the candidate's in-depth guide. The Champion meets the candidate for coffee or lunch. Jill Goldfine invites candidate to two upcoming YPO DC Learning Events (open to prospects through June 20 pre-summer). Candidate also attends a Prospective Member Luncheon or Dinner for a forum-like experience.

  • Note: No events available July 1–August 30 (summer break). Plan candidate timelines around this constraint.
  • Gate to Stage 5: All three touch-points (Champion meeting + 2 events + PM dinner) completed
  • Common failure modes: Candidate attends events but champion assignment is delayed; events are login-gated (send info directly to candidate)
Stage 5

Membership Committee Panel Interview

Owner: Chair (convenes panel) Duration: 15–20 minutes virtual; held on last Friday of each month Deliverable: Panel interview completed; committee recommendation memo drafted

The Membership Committee conducts a structured 15–20 minute virtual panel interview with the candidate. The panel assesses: vulnerability, engagement orientation, values alignment, and commitment to lifelong learning. Chair drafts a committee recommendation memo post-interview.

  • Panel composition: Chair + 2–3 committee members; Chair moderates
  • Key assessment criteria: Openness to vulnerability; peer-learning orientation (not just "taking"); fit with chapter culture (diversity, transformational leadership)
  • Gate to Stage 6: Committee recommendation = Proceed to Preboarding
  • Common failure modes: Scheduling conflicts delay month-end interview; committee quorum not achieved
Stage 6

YPO Global Preboarding

Owner: YPO Global (Champion Experience Team) Duration: 1-hour virtual call; small group format Deliverable: Preboarding completion confirmed with YPO Global

Mandatory call led by an experienced YPO Global Champion. Helps prospective members understand expectations before joining. The chapter makes the final membership decision, but no applicant can proceed to board approval without completing Preboarding.

  • Gate to Stage 7: Preboarding completion certificate from YPO Global
  • Common failure modes: Candidate doesn't schedule preboarding; call is missed; YPO Global scheduling backlog
Stage 7

Board Approval, Invoicing, and Onboarding

Owner: Chair (submits to board) + Chapter Director Lauren Feldman (onboarding) Duration: Board approval at next regular meeting; onboarding ongoing 12 months Deliverable: Board vote; invoice sent; forum placement; welcome packet

Chair submits a formal Board Approval Request memo. Board votes (typically at monthly operating meeting). Upon approval: invoice is sent for initiation fee (~$10,000) + annual dues (~$4,000–$5,300 YPO DC); YPO Global notified; new member onboarded; forum placement arranged (Markus Weiss leads forum management).

  • Onboarding milestones: Welcome letter from Chair; forum placement within 60 days; new member introduction at first chapter event; 12-month integration tracking
  • Common failure modes: Forum placement delayed; new member goes dark after joining; no structured integration check-ins
  • Chair action: Send Welcome Letter; introduce new member to their forum moderator; check in at 30, 90, and 180 days

C2. Proposed Enhancements

Enhancement 1 — Real-Time Candidate Status Dashboard

Effort: M Saves: 1–2 hrs/week Dep: None — builds on existing cron

Upgrade the weekly backlog cron to generate a live HTML dashboard with: visual stage pipeline (Stage 1–7 columns), days-in-stage indicator for each candidate (with amber/red SLA alerts), sponsor contact info, next action owner, and deadline countdown. Accessible at a fixed URL on Railway.

  • SLA alerts: Stage 2 GO/NO-GO >7 days = amber; >14 days = red
  • Stage 3 verification >30 days = amber flag
  • Any candidate with no activity for 21+ days = "stalled" badge

Enhancement 2 — Automated Sponsor Nudge Emails

Effort: M Saves: 30–60 min/week Dep: Gmail connector + tracker

When a candidate is stalled at any stage for >14 days, VM2 drafts a brief, warm email to the sponsor: "Quick update on [Candidate] — we're at [Stage] and the next step is [X]. Wanted to check in." Varun reviews and sends with one click. Eliminates sponsor follow-up lag.

Enhancement 3 — Monthly Committee Meeting Prep Auto-Pack

Effort: S Saves: 2–3 hrs/month Dep: Calendar event detection + tracker

30 minutes before each scheduled Membership Committee meeting, VM2 generates a one-page HTML packet: all active candidates (stage, days in stage, sponsor, next action), decisions needed at this meeting (GO/NO-GO votes, champion assignments, panel scheduling), and a draft agenda. Delivered to Varun's inbox and available at a Railway URL.

Enhancement 4 — Voice Memo → Vetting Call Notes Intake

Effort: S Saves: 20–30 min per call Dep: Varun records 60–90 sec memo; emails to vm2.viru@gmail.com

After each intro/vetting meeting, Varun records a brief voice note covering: candidate name, GO/NO-GO decision, key observations (age, role, motivation, red flags), and any commitments made to sponsor. VM2 receives the audio, transcribes it, extracts structured fields, updates the tracker, and drafts the follow-up communication (sponsor acknowledgement or next-steps email). Varun reviews and approves.

Enhancement 5 — Quarterly Board Report Auto-Draft

Effort: S Saves: 2–4 hrs/quarter Dep: Tracker data + board meeting calendar event

10 days before each quarterly board meeting, VM2 generates a draft Quarterly Membership Report from tracker data: pipeline summary (candidates by stage), quarter conversions (new approvals), attrition/renewals, committee activity, strategic notes, and recommended board actions. Varun edits and presents.

Enhancement 6 — 12-Month New Member Integration Tracker

Effort: M Saves: Prevents churn ($10K+ initiation fee at risk per member) Dep: Board approval trigger from tracker

When a new member is board-approved, VM2 opens an integration tracker entry with scheduled check-in reminders: welcome letter sent (Day 0), forum placement confirmed (Day 30), first event attended (Day 45), 90-day check-in call (Day 90), 6-month engagement review (Day 180), 12-month retention flag (Day 365). Varun is prompted for each touchpoint with a one-sentence status update.

Enhancement 7 — YPO Connect Nightly Sync Mirror

Effort: L Saves: Manual status checks; ensures tracker accuracy Dep: YPO Connect API access (to be confirmed with YPO Global)

Nightly automated check of YPO Connect chapter portal — pulls candidate status changes, new applications, verification completions, and preboarding confirmations into the local tracker. Flags any discrepancy between local tracker and YPO Connect. This is the highest-dependency enhancement and requires YPO Global API/export access. Confirm feasibility with concierge@ypo.org.

Recommended build order: Start with Enhancement 3 (meeting prep pack — smallest effort, immediate value), then Enhancement 4 (voice memo intake — changes Varun's daily workflow), then Enhancement 1 (dashboard upgrade — high visibility). Enhancements 5 and 6 follow naturally from the improved tracker.
