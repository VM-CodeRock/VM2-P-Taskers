6. Set-Aside Analysis & Advocacy Recommendation

FAA's Set-Aside Question

The RFI explicitly asks industry whether "adequate small business competition" exists — this is the statutory threshold under the Small Business Act that triggers a mandatory set-aside if two or more responsible small businesses are likely to submit offers. This creates a direct advocacy opportunity.

Recommended Advocacy Position for RFI Response

  • Assert WOSB/EDWOSB adequacy: Changeis should affirmatively state in its RFI response that adequate WOSB/EDWOSB competition exists, naming itself and at least one other qualifying firm. This directly supports a WOSB/EDWOSB restriction.
  • Provide market evidence: Reference NAICS 541219 HigherGov data ($25M size standard; active small business ecosystem) and eFAST MOA holder list (which includes multiple WOSB firms).
  • Frame the set-aside as mission-effective: Small businesses, particularly those with eFAST eligibility, offer the speed and flexibility FAA's Office of Financial Services requires for task order issuance. CEXEC's EFS I track record demonstrates small business adequacy.
  • Do not explicitly oppose full-and-open: Focus on affirmative arguments for small business competition rather than appearing to limit FAA's options. Let the advocacy speak through demonstrated capability.
