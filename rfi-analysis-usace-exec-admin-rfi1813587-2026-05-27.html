1 Executive Summary

This analysis evaluates RFI1813587, a U.S. Army Corps of Engineers (USACE) Sacramento District (SPK) requirement for Executive Administrative Support Services issued through GSA eBuy under the OASIS+ Unrestricted vehicle (contract 47QRCA25DU072). The RFI was posted May 22, 2026, with market research responses due June 1, 2026 at 5:00 PM EDT.

Three attachments were analyzed: the draft Performance Work Statement (PWS, dated April 29, 2026), the GSA MRAS Market Research document, and the GSA eBuy download record. The requirement calls for on-site administrative staff at 1325 J Street, Sacramento CA 95814 supporting the District Commander, Deputy District Engineer, and Chief of Staff across three functional areas: executive office admin (40%), training coordination (30%), and meeting/event support (30%).

Key Decision Points for Alex Madison / Go-No-Go

The core question is whether administrative/clerical services align with Changeis's CMMI Level 3 / IT-engineering positioning. The BD-RAG returned a composite score of 0.32 (LOW), confirming mismatch with Changeis past performance. A teaming strategy as EDWOSB sub under an admin-services prime is the recommended path.
