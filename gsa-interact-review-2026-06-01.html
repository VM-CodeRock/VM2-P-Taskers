High Priority Items
HIGH PRIORITY — OASIS+
  • OASIS+ Phase II Rolling Awards Active (May 12 / May 22, 2026): GSA's Office of Acquisition Solutions Development posted Phase II rolling apparent awardee announcements on SAM.gov on May 12, 2026. Formal awards and Notices to Proceed were planned on or about May 22, 2026, following final legal reviews. The vehicle remains continuously open with indefinite future solicitation windows. Changeis must confirm its award status across all relevant domains and initiate task-order readiness activities immediately. Apparent award is not contract activation — compliance hygiene, SAM.gov accuracy, teaming confirmations, and domain-specific positioning must all be in place before agency task orders issue.
HIGH PRIORITY — MAS / GSAR
  • MAS Pricing 2.0 Algorithm Goes Live June 5, 2026: GSA is replacing its market threshold algorithm for the Multiple Award Schedule program effective June 5. Two key changes: (1) the market baseline will now use the lesser of the current average or the minimum observed commercial price; (2) the price proportional premium is cut from 50% to a maximum of 25%, scaling down to a flat 5% above $5,000. The FAS Catalog Platform Compliance and Pricing Report will auto-calculate under the new rules. Changeis MAS contract holders offering COTS products must audit their pricing before June 5 — items that cleared the old threshold may fail the new one. Services-only and own-manufactured-products holders are not impacted.
  • GSAR Overhaul Underway (FY 2026): GSA launched a comprehensive GSAR Overhaul pursuant to Executive Order 14275 (Restoring Common Sense to Federal Procurement) and OMB Memorandum M-25-26. GSA is releasing deviations throughout FY 2026 via GSA Memorandum RGO-2026-01. Regulatory GSAR content is being separated from non-regulatory policy, with the latter moving to a new GSA Acquisition Handbook. All contractors and offerors are affected as the regulatory language governing GSA-awarded contracts evolves.
HIGH PRIORITY — 8(a) STARS
  • SBA 8(a) Program Audit — 1,000 Firms Suspended: The SBA's comprehensive 8(a) compliance audit (initiated December 2025, deadline January 5, 2026) resulted in approximately 1,000 of 4,300 active firms being suspended. No new tribal 8(a) entities have been approved since August 2025. The audit examines subcontracting limits, mentor-protégé performance, owner withdrawals, community benefit distributions, and fraud indicators. Any 8(a) STARS III teaming partners or subcontractors in Changeis's pipeline should be vetted for active certification status before proposal submission.
Items to Watch
Watch — FAS Reorganization
  • GSA FAS Reorganizes into Five Portfolios (May 26, 2026): The Federal Acquisition Service restructured into five new portfolios — Assist, Centralize, Create, Deliver, and Optimize — replacing the previous category-management organizational model. The reorganization consolidates previously disparate services and is intended to better align how agencies buy and how industry sells. Changeis business development and capture teams should map existing contacts and relationships to the new FAS portfolio structure to ensure BD outreach reaches the right contracting offices.
  • Proposed GSA AI Clause GSAR 552.239-7001: GSA's draft "Basic Safeguarding of Artificial Intelligence Systems" clause (published March 6, 2026) remains a live risk. Four provisions carry significant impact: an order-of-precedence clause making GSAR 552.239-7001 supreme over commercial terms of service; an "American AI" requirement restricting non-U.S.-developed AI components; broad IP provisions assigning government ownership of all data inputs, outputs, and custom developments; and an "Unbiased AI" mandate with undisclosed benchmarking. OMB M-26-04 declares compliance material to contract eligibility. MAS holders will have 60 days to accept once Refresh 31 issues. Changeis should begin an AI tool inventory now if not already completed.
  • IBM Awarded GSA FAS Enterprise Operations Solutions Support (EOSS) — May 28, 2026: IBM received a $5.68M, approximately 18-month contract under GSA MAS (Award ID 47QFEA26F0007) to support GSA FAS Systems Transformation. This indicates continued GSA investment in modernizing acquisition systems — relevant context for Changeis's IT and professional services positioning under MAS and OASIS+.
Watch — Construction / PBS
  • GSA 1800 F Building Modernization Industry Day — June 2, 2026: GSA Public Buildings Service hosted an industry day (June 2, 10am–3:30pm, 1800 F St NW Washington DC; virtual option available) for the upcoming 1800 F Building Revitalization Design-Bid-Build / Construction-Manager-as-Constructor contract. Two-phase RFP: Phase I qualifications expected July; Phase II pricing expected October. Award basis: best value. Sol# 47PB5426N0002. Not directly in Changeis's core lanes but signals active PBS capital construction pipeline.
