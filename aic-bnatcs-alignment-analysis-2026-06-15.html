Executive Summary
  • AIC is a newly-formed industry advocacy nonprofit launched May 7, 2026, chaired by Gene Hayman (Stratify Aerospace). It exists to give industry a coordinated voice specifically on BNATCS / NAS modernization, working in concert with the 56-member Modern Skies Coalition while maintaining its own separate governance, bylaws, and working groups. AIC's role is to translate industry expertise into actionable policy and technical recommendations for FAA and DOT.
  • Sen. Cruz's LAN26214 is a Senate legislative amendment targeting BNATCS statutory language. Based on context from the forwarded email, AIC received the amendment from Cruz's office alongside Modern Skies, reviewed it, and produced proposed edits focused on keeping the amendment tightly scoped to BNATCS success — explicitly rejecting scope creep. The full text and AIC's proposed language changes were in four PDF attachments that are no longer accessible from the signed URLs in the email.
  • Changeis is directly in the blast radius of BNATCS legislation. As the CLMRS incumbent ($62M) and ATAPS II holder ($103M), Changeis is embedded in the FAA ATO directorates — including the Program Management Organization (AJM) — that directly manage BNATCS programs. Any statute shaping BNATCS scope, funding, and acquisition authorities will affect the work Changeis does and bids for.
  • The AIC briefing on June 13, 2026 included direct FAA AMO/NextGen/ANG and DOT staff (Groce, Fontaine, Crawford, Loring, Rebecca Guy), meaning AIC already has a direct engagement channel with the people who oversee Changeis's contracts. Varun's inclusion in the briefing circle suggests Changeis is seen as a relevant voice.
  • FAA alignment strategy: Changeis should support AIC's narrowly-scoped BNATCS amendment position (avoid scope creep, focus on execution success), engage AMO/ANG leadership directly, and use this moment to position Changeis as the execution-credible prime on any follow-on BNATCS support work created by additional $20B appropriation.
Aviation Innovation Coalition Profile

The Aviation Innovation Coalition was officially launched on May 7, 2026 in Washington, D.C. as a nonprofit, industry-led organization bringing together aviation, technology, infrastructure, and policy leaders to support FAA modernization of the National Airspace System (National Law Review, May 7, 2026).

Key Insight
AIC is distinct from Modern Skies Coalition: Modern Skies is a broad 56+ member coalition supporting general ATC modernization funding. AIC is a more focused, technically-oriented nonprofit with its own governance (bylaws, charter, OCI policies, antitrust policies, IP policies, membership agreements, working group charters) that can engage directly on specific amendment language — which is exactly what LAN26214 represents.

Key Insight — "Avoid Scope Creep"
AIC's explicit instruction to "avoid scope creep" in LAN26214 proposed language signals a deliberate choice to keep BNATCS tightly defined around the five-pillar technical upgrade (Communications, Surveillance, Automation, Facilities, Alaska) rather than allowing the amendment to expand into adjacent workforce, governance, or organizational reform mandates that would slow execution. For Changeis, this is favorable: tightly scoped BNATCS means faster acquisition vehicles and clearer SOW boundaries for support contracts.

The administration's #1 concern with BNATCS is execution risk — Secretary Duffy and Administrator Bedford have staked political capital on the 2028 deadline. Changeis's incumbency on CLMRS and ATAPS II is proof of execution, not just capability. In every FAA interaction and any AIC working group submission, Changeis should lead with this: "We are already executing on the NAS supply chain and ATO program management that BNATCS depends on."
