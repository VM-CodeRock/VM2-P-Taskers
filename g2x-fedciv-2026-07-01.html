CISA BOD 26-04 — Risk-Based Vulnerability Management Directive Now Driving Federal Cyber Demand; 3-Day Critical Patch Timeline for All FCEB Agencies
CISA's Binding Operational Directive 26-04 (issued June 10, 2026) replaces the legacy KEV catalog flat-deadline approach (BOD 22-01) with a four-variable risk-weighted model: asset exposure, KEV status, exploit automation, and technical impact. The most urgent tier requires remediation within 3 calendar days including forensic triage. The directive applies to all Federal Civilian Executive Branch (FCEB) agencies and extends to contractor-owned systems processing federal data. BOD 26-04 supersedes both BOD 22-01 and BOD 19-02. Simultaneously, OMB Memorandum M-26-14 (May 22, 2026) mandates new logging architecture with 6-month searchable and 12-month retrievable log retention. Combined, agencies have a roughly 5-month window (September–January 2027) to achieve full compliance with both mandates.
