DHS Jun 18, 2026 Cloud / Contract Award

OPM awarded Oracle a nearly $400M contract to replace over 100 legacy HR systems with Oracle Fusion Cloud HCM, targeting approximately 2 million federal civilian employees by 2028. This is one of the largest civilian workforce modernization awards of FY26.
NIH / GSA Jun 18, 2026 Vehicle Sunset / Policy
NIH to Sunset CIO-SP3/CIO-SP4; Contractors Shift to GSA Vehicles
NITAAC formally announced sunsetting CIO-SP4 and CIO-SP3 per Executive Order 14240 (Eliminating Waste). CIO-SP3 orders may not extend PoPs beyond Dec 31, 2028. Future NIH IT needs will migrate to GSA Schedule and other federal contract vehicles — a critical BD pivot point for incumbents and aspiring primes.
