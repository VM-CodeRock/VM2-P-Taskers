Pentagon Section 1260H / Section 805 NDAA — Now Binding
Effective Jun 30
!
Section 805 (FY2024 NDAA) Entity Ban — Effective June 30, 2026: As of yesterday, the Department of Defense is prohibited from entering into, renewing, or extending contracts with any entity on the Section 1260H List of Chinese Military Companies or entities subject to their control. The DoD published an updated 1260H List on June 8, 2026, designating 188 Chinese companies. Key designations relevant to aerospace/aviation supply chains include: AVIC subsidiaries (Align Aerospace LLC, Avicopter PLC, Cirrus Design Corporation / Cirrus Aircraft, Continental Aerospace Technologies), Alibaba, Baidu, BYD, NIO, Unitree Robotics, and WuXi AppTec. The Product Ban (Section 805) takes effect June 30, 2027 — prohibiting DoD contracts for goods/services that include products produced or developed by 1260H entities. Changeis BD and legal teams must audit any FAA/DoD teaming or subcontract arrangements for 1260H-listed company exposure in supply chains before July 2027. Sources: WilmerHale / TechTimes / DoD 1260H List PDF.
