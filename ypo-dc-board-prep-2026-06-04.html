2 · Membership Officer Role Brief

Core Mandate

  • Own the full membership lifecycle: introduction → application → vetting → board approval → forum placement → 12-month retention
  • Hold delegated authority from the Chapter Board on all membership decisions
  • GO/NO-GO authority after initial vet (sole); committee recommendation after panel (majority vote with tiebreak)
  • Present monthly membership report at Chapter Board Operating Meeting
  • Submit quarterly board report (pipeline metrics, conversions, attrition, strategic posture)

vs. Current Vice Chair Role

  • Vice Chair: Advisory/committee seat — attends meetings, conducts 1:1 intro calls, escalates to Chair
  • Membership Officer: Board officer with final authority — leads strategy, owns pipeline, presents to board, sets SMART goals, manages annual budget for membership function
  • Elevation means Varun is the Chair, not reporting to one
  • Responsible for recruiting and onboarding his own Vice Chair (Kiely Wilkerson under discussion)
  • Monthly check-in with YPO Global Champion Experience Team (concierge@ypo.org)
