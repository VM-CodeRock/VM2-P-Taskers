FAR Council / GSA Jun 23–29, 2026 FAR Overhaul / Formal Rulemaking / Batch 1
FAR Revolutionary Overhaul Enters Formal Rulemaking — Batch 1 Covers 21 Parts, New Part 40 Security Consolidation, Award Announcement Threshold Raised to $5.5M, Comment Deadline July 23
The FAR Council published four proposed rules in the Federal Register on June 23, 2026, marking the formal rulemaking transition of the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul. Batch 1 amends 21 FAR parts: new Part 40 consolidates supply chain risk management, security prohibitions, and CUI handling; Part 39 expands to information and communications technology broadly; Part 33 updates protest and debrief procedures; the mandatory public award announcement threshold rises from $4.5M to $5.5M.
