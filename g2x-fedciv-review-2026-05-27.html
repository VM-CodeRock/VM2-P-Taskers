DHS CBP · Office of Information and Technology

CBP BEAGLE SAILS Recompete — Sources Sought Closes June 1 High Signal

Est. $150M–$450M Posted May 21, 2026 · Closes June 1 Sources Sought / RFI Solicitation: CBP20153274 Single-Award BPA

CBP's Office of Information and Technology has issued a Sources Sought notice (CBP20153274) for the Border Enforcement & Management Systems & Applications Integration & Lifecycle Support (SAILS) — the full recompete of the BEAGLE contract. The scope encompasses application integration and lifecycle support, modernization, cloud migration, DevSecOps, data analytics, AI/ML implementation, cybersecurity support, and enterprise application management across CBP border enforcement systems. The environment spans legacy COBOL, .NET, Java, Python, JavaScript, Salesforce, SAP Business Objects, and ServiceNow platforms.

CBP intends to award a single-award BPA under FAR Part 8.4 and plans to release a secondary RFI specifically targeting prime offerors. Companies intending to compete as primes must express interest to Ryan Lastrella (ryan.lastrella@cbp.dhs.gov) and Virgil Rogers (virgil.m.rogers@cbp.dhs.gov) by June 1, 2026 at 4:00 PM EDT.

Changeis BD Relevance
This is the most time-sensitive opportunity in today's brief — prime intent is due June 1 (4 days). Changeis should assess subcontracting potential under a major prime and identify teaming partners with CBP border enforcement application experience. NAICS 541512 (Computer Systems Design Services) is the expected classification. The single-award structure means the prime slot is critical; teaming strategy should be finalized immediately.

Ryan Donaghy Named CISA's First Chief Operating Officer Watch

Announced May 22, 2026 Leadership Change CISA COO (new role)

CISA has appointed Ryan Donaghy as its first-ever Chief Operating Officer, a newly created role. Donaghy previously served in acting leadership capacities across two CISA divisions before moving to TSA in October. His return to CISA in the top operational role signals organizational restructuring and a potential consolidation of operational management across CISA's mission programs. Separately, House Homeland Security Democrats have requested a CISA briefing following reports of a publicly accessible GitHub repository connected to a government contractor that exposed CISA data — a contractor-side security incident that may accelerate vendor oversight requirements.

The CISA GitHub data leak controversy adds urgency to cybersecurity and contractor oversight programs. Monitor CISA procurement forecast for new management advisory, organizational transformation, and contractor performance programs. CISA currently has active forecasts: Vendor IT Research Licenses (Alliant 2, est. sol. June 2026) and Priority Comms End-to-End Integration (est. sol. June 2026, F2026072756).

DHS ICE · Immigration and Customs Enforcement

ICE Awards $25M No-Bid Iris Biometrics Contract to BI2 Technologies High Signal

$25.1M Awarded May 22, 2026 Award — Sole Source Contractor: BI2 Technologies

DHS ICE finalized a $25.1 million sole-source contract with Massachusetts-based BI2 Technologies for iris biometric recognition hardware, mobile applications, and database access to support field enforcement operations. The contract is over five times the size of BI2's prior DHS agreement and includes a request for more than 1,500 iris scanning devices plus access to BI2's proprietary biometric information system and mobile app for field agent use. ICE agents are deploying iris scanners in field operations including apartment raids and jails, with scans fed into a database for ongoing identification.

Privacy concerns raised by EFF, Georgetown Law Center on Privacy and Technology, and NPR center on database scope, aggregation with other biometric data (DNA, facial recognition), and the absence of congressional authorization for this surveillance expansion.

Changeis BD Relevance
The scale and controversy of this procurement signal that DHS/ICE biometrics programs will face increased congressional scrutiny and oversight requirements — creating demand for independent program assessments, privacy impact analysis, and policy advisory support. This is a major competitor/partner awareness signal: BI2's foothold at ICE will likely generate downstream integration, data management, and program management task orders. Firms supporting ICE identity programs should engage proactively. Changeis should assess advisory angles (program oversight, privacy compliance, IV&V) as follow-on opportunities emerge.
Sources: NPR — ICE Iris Scanners · SquaredTech — ICE BI2 Contract Analysis

USCIS Awards $254M+ Across Three Contracts; RIVER Protests Ongoing High Signal

$254M+ combined May 26, 2026 Awards + Active Protests
