FedScoop Article Summary — FAA AI/ATC Modernization

Produced 2026-06-26 · VM2 One-Shot Task ID: 6gx9J5fqHvQQJgr9 · Changeis Strategy & Intelligence
Executive Summary
  • On June 23, 2026, the FAA awarded Air Space Intelligence (ASI) a 12-year, nearly $876 million contract to build two AI-driven platforms — Flow Management Data Services (FMDS) and Strategic Management of Airspace, Routes, and Trajectories (SMART) — marking the most significant software procurement in the FAA's ongoing ATC overhaul.
  • FMDS will become the "new technological backbone" of the FAA's Air Traffic Control System Command Center, proactively identifying delays by centralizing and processing airspace data at scale.
  • SMART, an AI tool embedded within FMDS, will enable controllers to overlay weather patterns into a single visualization and strategically coordinate schedules and trajectories before aircraft depart — targeting a fall 2026 initial operations date.
  • Peraton, the FAA's prime integrator ($12.5B BNATCS contract), is deploying agentic AI internally — analyzing 5.7 million schedule and resource records — while simultaneously coordinating 52 vendors on the broader modernization program.
  • Total FAA modernization funding: $12.5B already secured from Congress, $4B proposed for FY2027, and an FAA ask of $20B total to complete the three-year, NAS-wide overhaul by Q3 2027.

Key Takeaways
  1. $876M ASI Contract Awarded (June 23, 2026): Air Space Intelligence won a 12-year contract to build FMDS and SMART — both fully deployed within 24 months. This is the largest AI/software award of the modernization program to date.
  2. FMDS as New NAS Backbone: Flow Management Data Services will centralize demand-capacity balancing and traffic management data at the FAA's Air Traffic Control System Command Center, replacing legacy data silos.
  3. SMART AI Tool — Fall 2026 Launch: Strategic Management of Airspace, Routes, and Trajectories will give controllers a unified weather-and-trajectory visualization layer. Initial operations target: fall 2026.
  4. Peraton Prime — Agentic AI at Scale: Peraton is using agentic AI to manage its role as prime integrator — analyzing 1.5M records per day, stress-testing schedules, flagging supply chain gaps, and surfacing risks before they become crises. 5.7M schedule/resource records analyzed to date.
  5. Digital Twins of the NAS Built: Using 20+ years of FAA data, vendors created digital twins of the entire National Airspace System in a matter of months — enabling predictive analytics that deconflict and optimize future schedules.
  6. FAA Funding Gap: Congress provided $12.5B (FY2025); White House proposed $4B more (FY2027); but the FAA says it needs $20B total. The project is partially unfunded — FAA Administrator Bedford and Transportation Secretary Duffy are actively lobbying Congress.
  7. Infrastructure Progress: ~50% of copper wires replaced, ~270 radio sites converted, 54 airports with new surface awareness systems, 17 towers on electronic flight strips — all since the overhaul announcement in 2025.
  8. Bedford's "Third Wave" Framing: FAA Administrator Bryan Bedford described AI integration as the "third wave of true modernization" — following infrastructure replacement and software upgrades.
  9. UAS Integration Pressure: Airport/airline stakeholders are pushing for counter-UAS capabilities and ADS-B In mandates simultaneously. The FAA's new drone office (stood up January 2026) is finalizing rules, none yet effective.
  10. Target Completion: Q3 2027 — FAA confirmed to Congress the modernization program is expected complete by Q3 2027. Peraton's contract includes delay penalties as an on-time incentive.
