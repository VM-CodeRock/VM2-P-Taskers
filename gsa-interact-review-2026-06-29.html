High Priority Items
URGENT — MAS TDR / Modification A909 — Deadline June 30, 2026
  • Transactional Data Reporting Becomes Mandatory July 1, 2026 — Accept Mod A909 by June 30: GSA is expanding TDR across all MAS contracts effective July 1, 2026. All current MAS contract holders must accept Mass Modification A909 in the eMod portal before June 30, 2026. Delayed acceptance past June 30 triggers immediate contract termination. Once accepted, TDR replaces Most Favored Customer (MFC) pricing disclosure requirements and eliminates the commercial sales practices disclosure. The shift also changes reporting from quarterly to monthly via the FAS Sales Reporting Portal. For services contracts: UCID (Unique Contract Item Descriptor) must be a one-to-one match to labor categories in the approved GSA price list catalog. Firms transitioning to TDR from October 1, 2025 forward have a grace period through December 31, 2026 to resolve compliance issues, but contract acceptance itself cannot wait. GSA held a MAS PMO Office Hours session on TDR on June 10, 2026 (recording available on YouTube). If Changeis holds any MAS contract, check eMod today and accept Modification A909 immediately. Source: Fed Services, GSA MAS PMO Office Hours (YouTube, June 10, 2026)
HIGH PRIORITY — MAS Refresh 32 (Mass Modification A917) — Deadline ~July 4, 2026
  • GSA Issued MAS Refresh 32 on June 4, 2026 — 30-Day Acceptance Window: GSA issued Mass Modification A917 to MAS Solicitation 47QSMD20R0001 (MAS Refresh 32) on June 4, 2026. Key changes include: (1) FAR 52.222-90 (DEI Certification) — MAS contract holders must certify they do not operate DEI programs that discriminate in violation of applicable federal law. This clause applies to both existing and future orders and BPA calls. False certification exposes contractors to False Claims Act liability including treble damages. Executive Order 11246 (affirmative action requirements) has been rescinded by the current administration. Legal review of existing affirmative action plans, supplier diversity programs, ERGs, and diversity-focused recruiting is strongly advised before certification. (2) New SIN 518210GM (Grants Management Solutions and Services) launched in partnership with HHS under Refresh 32 — relevant to professional services firms serving grant-heavy agencies. (3) Drone/UAS language updates and ICT end-of-support considerations. (4) SIN 311423 retirement. Contractors have 30 days from issuance (approximately July 4, 2026) to accept in the mass modification system. Non-acceptance places the MAS contract in non-compliant status, blocking new task orders. Source: GovConFeed, FEDSched
HIGH PRIORITY — OASIS+ Phase II Formal Awards and NTPs Active
  • OASIS+ Phase II Formal Awards Issued on or About May 22, 2026: GSA's Office of Acquisition Solutions Development issued Phase II rolling apparent awardee announcements on SAM.gov on May 12, 2026, and issued formal awards and Notices to Proceed (NTPs) to qualified contractors on or about May 22, 2026 following final legal reviews. The OASIS+ solicitation remains continuously open — new solicitation windows allow firms to enter future phases. All new professional services task orders must go through OASIS+; the legacy OASIS vehicles are fully closed (Unrestricted ordering period ended March 1, 2025). Apparent award is not revenue: task-order business development begins at NTP. Changeis must confirm award status across all submitted OASIS+ domains, initiate task-order readiness, verify SAM.gov registration accuracy, and begin agency outreach. Source: GSA OASIS+ official page, GovConFeed OASIS+ Guide 2026
HIGH PRIORITY — Buy American Act RFI — June 28, 2026
  • GSA Issues Buy American Act RFI Signaling Two-Tier GSA Advantage! Marketplace: GSA published a Buy American Act RFI on June 28, 2026 that signals the agency is considering separating domestic-content items from standard Schedule listings on GSA Advantage! — a potential "two-tier display" framework. If adopted, contractors offering products must prove origin, sourcing, and Trade Agreements Act compliance at the SKU level, not merely at the company or contract level. $40B+ in annual MAS sales are potentially exposed to domestic-content screening. Noncompliant SKUs can be removed from GSA Advantage! within 30–60 days of a correction request. Compliance budget estimates run $15,000–$75,000 depending on catalog complexity. Recommended action within 30 days: inventory every SKU, collect supplier affidavits and bills of materials, reconcile catalog data between Schedule file and Advantage! listings. The practical response deadline (30 days from June 28 RFI signal) is July 28, 2026. Services-only MAS holders are minimally affected; product resellers and distributors face the highest compliance burden. Source: Gov Contract Finder (June 28, 2026)

  • FAST GSA 2026 Virtual Acquisition Summit — June 16, 2026 (Materials Available): GSA hosted the FAST GSA 2026 Virtual Acquisition Summit for Industry on June 16, 2026 (9 a.m.–4 p.m. ET). Key sessions covered: Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO) — changes to FAR Part 19 and acquisition strategy compliance; MAS reform and TDR expansion; Procurement Consolidation per Executive Order 14240 and OMB Implementation Plan (focusing on governmentwide IDVs and Assisted/Centralized Acquisition through GSA); GSA FAS Office of Centralized Acquisition Services (OCAS) transition; Office of Small Business guidance on 8(a) sole-source contracting in Q4; SAM.gov system updates; and GSA's use of AI in acquisition. Presentation slides (PPTX) are available on the event page. Review the MAS reform, TDR, and procurement consolidation slides as they contain actionable compliance guidance. Source: GSA.gov FAST Summit 2026
  • Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO) — Ongoing: GSA and OMB are pursuing a broad FAR simplification effort. Changes to FAR Part 19 (small business) are expected. The overhaul includes modifications to ordering procedures under FAR Part 8 / GSAR 538.71, changes to BPA approvals (HCA approval threshold for single BPAs over $100M eliminated), removal of lowest-cost alternative language in favor of best value, and elimination of the OLM percentage cap. GSAR deviations continue to be released throughout FY 2026 under Memorandum RGO-2026-01. Monitor acquisition.gov/gsar-overhaul for new releases. Source: GSA FAST Summit FY26 Day 1 materials
  • Procurement Consolidation — EO 14240 Implementation Active: OMB's procurement consolidation plan (EO 14240) is restructuring federal IT buying toward governmentwide vehicles and shared services. Workstream 1 focuses on Governmentwide IDVs and Shared Services; Workstream 2 on Assisted and Centralized Acquisition through GSA. For industry, this emphasizes existing government-wide contract vehicles. eBuy SIN identification may be used for administrative purposes but does not restrict MAS scope. Changeis should ensure its GSA vehicles are positioned in SAM.gov and eBuy for maximum visibility under the consolidating marketplace. Source: GSA FAST Summit 2026
