Google DeepMind's Gemini 3.5 Pro now has a reported July 17 target date following an architecture rebuild, and xAI's Grok 4.5 continues its private-beta run at SpaceX and Tesla with vendor claims of near-Opus performance at a fraction of the cost (Tech Times).

Anthropic / Claude
Federal Signal
CISA Deploys Claude Mythos to Scan Government Code for Hidden Flaws

Reuters reports that CISA's Attack Surface Evaluation team — the unit that tests federal computer systems for exploitable gaps — is running Anthropic's Claude Mythos model against government software, with the scans already uncovering a substantial number of bugs. The National Security Agency has separately been testing Mythos as well. The deployment underscores growing federal reliance on Anthropic's most capable model even as the company's relationship with Washington remains tense, and comes one week after the White House lifted export controls that had forced a temporary global suspension of Mythos and Fable 5 (The Daily Star / Reuters).

Security Disclosure
China Flags Claude Code Data-Handling Concerns; Anthropic Ships Sandboxed Browser

Chinese authorities said several versions of Claude Code can transmit information such as user location and identity to remote servers without explicit consent, attributing this to a built-in monitoring mechanism — the latest entry in ongoing cross-border scrutiny of Anthropic's coding tools (CNBC). Separately, Anthropic introduced a sandboxed browser inside the Claude desktop app for Claude Code, letting the agent access websites, launch local dev servers, take screenshots, and validate UI changes without switching tools — a step toward a fuller end-to-end autonomous coding agent.

Deadline
Claude Fable 5 Included Access Window Closes July 12Deadline Tonight

Anthropic's extended included-access window for Claude Fable 5 — its Mythos-class flagship model — closes July 12 at 11:59:59 PM PT, after which Fable 5 usage shifts to prepaid credits only ($10/$50 per million input/output tokens) with no automatic fallback to another model. The 50%-of-weekly-limit included allowance and covered plans (Pro, Max, Team, seat-based Enterprise) are unchanged from the original July 1 restoration terms; Standard Enterprise and API tiers were never included (Digital Applied).

OpenAI
Litigation
Apple Sues OpenAI Over 400+ Poached Employees, Alleges Trade-Secret Theft

Apple filed suit against OpenAI and two former employees in Northern California federal court on July 11, alleging misappropriation of trade secrets tied to OpenAI's push into consumer hardware. The complaint highlights that more than 400 former Apple employees now work at OpenAI, many from Apple's chip design, hardware, and on-device AI teams, and Apple is seeking a jury trial along with an order for OpenAI to re-engineer any hardware allegedly built on Apple's technology (AP News, WSJ, New York Post). The suit lands days before OpenAI's Codex hardware device is expected to launch on July 15.

IPO
OpenAI Reportedly Preparing Confidential IPO Filing, ~$730B Valuation Target

OpenAI is said to be preparing a confidential IPO filing with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley, aiming to go public as soon as September 2026 at a valuation of roughly $730 billion — which would be the largest technology IPO in history if it holds (Unrot).

Model Rollout
GPT-5.6 Tiers (Sol/Terra/Luna) Settle Into Distinct Roles Post-Launch

Two days after its government-cleared public rollout, GPT-5.6's three tiers have sorted into clear use cases: Terra ($2.50/$15 per million tokens) is the value pick, scoring 84.3% on Terminal-Bench 2.1 — roughly matching Claude Fable 5 at half the cost; Sol ($5/$30) is the top-end performer at 88.8% (91.9% in Ultra mode); and Luna ($1/$6) handles cheap, high-volume work (Unrot).

Perplexity
Federal Partnership
Perplexity Enterprise Pro for Government Live via GSA Multiple Award Schedule

Perplexity's newly announced U.S. government partnership makes Enterprise Pro for Government available to federal agencies through GSA's Multiple Award Schedule (MAS IT) at essentially no cost for the first 18 months, with long-term pricing commitments locked in. Perplexity says this makes it the first major AI company to enter a direct government-wide contract; all federal-agency requests also receive automatic zero-data-use protections and access to Perplexity's most advanced models by default, with no subscription required (Perplexity). The offering builds on Perplexity's AI Prioritization designation, one of only two government-designated services, which expedites its FedRAMP authorization via the cloud-native 20x pathway.

Product Launch
Comet Browser Now Free Worldwide; Mobile and Background Assistants Expand

Perplexity released its Comet browser free to everyone worldwide, following a waitlist-gated limited release that became "the most sought-after AI product of the year" per the company. Perplexity also introduced Background Assistants — a platform of asynchronous AI assistants that work across browser, inbox, and background tasks — alongside continued Comet mobile rollout to Android and iOS (Perplexity).

Google DeepMind / xAI / Meta
Model Launch
Gemini 3.5 Pro Targets July 17 After Ground-Up Architecture Rebuild

Google DeepMind is reportedly targeting July 17 for Gemini 3.5 Pro's general availability after scrapping its original 2.5 Pro-derived base model in favor of a deeper pre-training cycle. The rebuilt model is said to feature a 2-million-token context window, double Gemini 2.5 Pro's cap, plus a Deep Think reasoning layer for multi-step logic. As of this week, Google has not officially confirmed pricing or a model card, so the date should be treated as a reported target rather than a locked commitment (Tech Times).

Model / Coding
Grok 4.5 Remains in Private Beta at SpaceX and Tesla, Public API Live

xAI's Grok 4.5 — a 1.5-trillion-parameter model trained on Cursor coding data and built for coding, agentic tasks, and knowledge work — is available on the xAI API at $2/$6 per million input/output tokens with configurable reasoning effort, while broader internal testing continues at SpaceX and Tesla. Third-party benchmarking from Artificial Analysis rates it second only to Claude Opus 4.8 and GPT-5.5, ahead of Claude Sonnet 5 and GLM-5.2, though vendor claims of "near-Opus" performance remain independently unverified at full scale (Releasebot / xAI).

Infrastructure
Meta Stock Jumps on AI Chip and Compute Buildout Plans

Meta shares rose sharply after a Reuters report that the company plans to begin manufacturing its own AI chip, codenamed "Iris," with partners Broadcom and TSMC starting in September, part of a plan to expand total computing capacity to 14 gigawatts next year. Meta also confirmed a $13 billion (roughly $9.1B USD) AI data center in Alberta, Canada — its first in the country (Mint / Reuters, AP News).

Federal AI / Policy / Macro
Fed / Macro
Federal Reserve Names Marc Andreessen to Co-Lead New AI Task Force

The Federal Reserve appointed venture capitalist and a16z co-founder Marc Andreessen to co-lead a new task force studying how AI affects jobs, productivity, and monetary policy — the first time the central bank has built a formal body specifically around AI's economic impact. The move follows the Fed's July 10 monetary policy report to Congress, which cited the AI infrastructure buildout as a contributor to inflation stepping up this spring, alongside tariffs and war-related energy costs (Unrot, Reuters).

Enforcement
CISA Orders Urgent Patching of Langflow Agent Platform Flaw

CISA added CVE-2026-55255 — an access-control flaw in the Langflow visual agent-building framework — to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, directing federal agencies to patch on a tight timeline. The flaw, an insecure direct object reference in the /api/v1/responses endpoint, has already been exploited to steal AI and cloud credentials from affected agent deployments (AI Agent Store).

Government Use Case
HHS to Use ChatGPT for Medicare/Medicaid Fraud Audits

The U.S. Department of Health and Human Services announced it will use ChatGPT to analyze audit reports from all 50 states, hunting for fraud and waste across roughly $2.1 trillion in annual Medicare and Medicaid spending — one of the largest single federal use cases for a commercial AI model disclosed to date (Unrot).

Agent Tooling & Enterprise AI
Coding Tools
Perplexity's Internal "Teammate" Coding Agent Signals Possible Public Launch

Business Insider reports Perplexity has built an internal, model-agnostic AI coding tool codenamed "Teammate," used by engineers since May for long-horizon engineering work such as owning projects, investigating issues, and monitoring services. A public launch date is unconfirmed, but the tool would put Perplexity in more direct competition with Cursor, Anthropic's Claude Code, and OpenAI's Codex in the AI coding tools market (Business Insider).
